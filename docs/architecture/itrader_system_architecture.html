Solver Frontier

SolverWindowLSTMFiLM is documented as an in-development Phase B candidate only. It should be opt-in, feed-forward PPO compatible, and compared against SolverMLPFiLM on identical seeds and task pools before promotion.

Phase C Shadow Loop

Market Windowlive or replayed context
CapabilityProfilecurrent solver range by family, regime, horizon
MarketFramerframes capability-conditioned trade and WAIT candidates
Solverscores solvability, edge, confidence

MarketFramer is capability-aware: it should shape candidate difficulty around the solver heads' current Goldilocks band. Final approval remains deterministic after solver scoring, threshold checks, and risk review.

AB Lens
