03 · Typed language

Contracts prevent cleverness from becoming ambiguity.

Training problems, possible trades, empirical competence and final outcomes are different concepts. iTrader keeps them different on purpose.

The canonical problem statement: family, horizon, bar resolution, reward configuration, difficulty and market-window reference.

Offline + solver

How a valid candidate could be traded: side, entry, stop, take-profit, TTL and sizing hints. It does not grant execution.

Empirical solver competence indexed by family, regime, horizon and calibration. This defines the moving Goldilocks band.

The audit object for candidate set, solver scores, gate/risk decisions, final action and realized outcome.

04 · Offline learning

A curriculum that must earn its usefulness.

Phase B establishes a frozen proposer, reliable solver evidence and the data needed to define r_prop. Phase C activates proposer reward and closes the co-adaptive loop so held-out evaluation can determine whether either side actually improved.

Current train_AZR.py implements the bootstrap seam: generate a validated task pool, then hand it to the stable solver trainer. Phase B makes that frozen-proposer path measurable and designs r_prop; Phase C implements the complete rewarded feedback loop.

Proposer evolution

Freeze first, optimize after evidence.

Phase B implements a frozen proposer with validated, family-aware structured output and deterministic fallbacks; LoRA is deliberately out of the critical path. Phase C closes the r_prop loop and tests it properly before later adapter or grouped-candidate optimization.

Goldilocks principle

Neither trivial nor impossible.

A useful task creates learnable signal, positive utility after costs, acceptable stability and measurable solver uplift. Win rate alone is not difficulty, capability or value.

05 · Solver + reward

Learned judgment, explicit evidence.

The solver is the learned market-control component, not the broker and not the risk authority. Its outputs become useful only when reward components, calibration and held-out behavior remain visible.

Implemented control

SolverMLPFiLM

The flattened price window enters an MLP trunk. A task embedding modulates hidden features through FiLM before policy logits and value estimation. This remains the reference head for all ablations.

Phase B candidates

Window LSTM + stateful FiLM

R1 reshapes only the price slice and resets each decision. R2 carries RLlib state across steps. Both are unimplemented and must be compared separately; the newest input is the final row of the chronological window.

06 · Online shell

Capability-aware framing, deterministic permission.

MarketFramer may propose. Solver heads may score. Neither may trade. Every path to action crosses inspectable thresholds and a final rule-based risk boundary; WAIT is a successful decision when evidence is weak.

Replay → shadow → live decision cycle

Contracts present · runtime planned
Rolling windowLive or replayed market evidence and portfolio state.
Capability queryWhat each solver head can handle now.
MarketFramer1–3 Goldilocks candidates plus WAIT.
Solver scoringSolvability, edge, value and confidence.
DecisionGateDeterministic validation, thresholds and ranking.
RiskEngineExposure, drawdown, costs, cooldown and kill switch.
Executor + recordSim/shadow/live action and complete audit trail.

The key distinction: MarketFramer uses both the market window and CapabilityProfile to shape task difficulty around a sliding solver-specific Goldilocks band. DecisionGate, not MarketFramer, makes the final deterministic selection after scoring.

DecisionGate

Inspectable chooser

Reject invalid or low-confidence candidates, require positive expected utility, rank surviving options and prefer WAIT when nothing clears the configured bar.

RiskEngine

Final authority

Spread, slippage, size, exposure, loss, drawdown, cooldown, session windows and kill switches remain deterministic and cannot be overridden.

Executor

Faithful mechanism

Translate approved intent into sim, shadow or broker actions; track acknowledgements, fills and failures; never invent strategy or alter risk rules.

External orchestration

WatsonOW may call iTrader later; it never becomes the trading authority.

WatsonOW can request analysis, replay or evaluation, summarize evidence and maintain memory. It cannot bypass validation, DecisionGate, RiskEngine or Executor semantics.

Knowledge layer

The local graph, MCP tools and generated wiki accelerate architecture exploration and impact checks. Freshness must be checked, and code/tests remain authoritative when graph and source disagree.

09 · Architectural constitution

The rules that survive model changes.

Architectures age well when their boundaries are stronger than their current implementation. These are the project’s durable decisions.

01

Extend the working path before rewriting it. Family 00 and the FiLM baseline are controls, not legacy clutter.

02

Keep typed contracts central. Task, execution, capability and decision records remain distinct.

03

Reward stays explicit, finite and decomposed. Training return alone never proves capability.

04

Capability is empirical and sliced by family, regime, horizon and calibration.

05

WAIT is first-class. Abstention is often the most intelligent online output.

06

LLMs propose; deterministic systems validate, gate, constrain and execute.

07

Replay precedes shadow; shadow precedes live. Risk decisions remain final.

08

Prefer clear family heads and ablations before MoE, persistent recurrence or capacity tricks.

09

Every experiment must be explainable later through manifests, metrics, datasets and records.
