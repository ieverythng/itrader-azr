R0 + R4-v1 actor-robustness audit

Date: 2026-09-29
Status: complete; mechanically valid search, no economic promotion

Decision under test

The accepted feed-forward baseline remains R0 + R4-v1: the MLP-FiLM market/task path plus the minimal agent-visible position and remaining-horizon state residual. State-free R0 remains the immutable parity control. The combined state arm is architecture-qualified but economically unpromoted; this audit tests whether its actor is more robust under lower learning rates, bounded entropy pressure, and a causal transaction-cost curriculum.

This is not an R4-v2, R1, task-family, proposer, data, or reward-function experiment. No market/task x state interaction is added. Family 00, the random/generated proposer, dataset-v2 RTH partitions, the 60x5 market input, semantic-v2 task encoding, exact 1 bp evidence, and the Ray 2.58 legacy PPO stack remain fixed.

Frozen experiment contract

The canonical loadout is phase_a_r0_plus_r4_v1_actor_robustness_pbt_4x40_20260929.json. Its primary arm uses:

  • combined position + remaining_horizon_fraction state with zero_v1 residual initialization;
  • four synchronous PBT members, two resident local-worker trials at a time, and a 40-update / approximately 192k-step cap per member;
  • nonflat_net donor ranking with the absolute economic gate kept separate;
  • learning rate bounded to 5e-6 .. 6e-5;
  • the four frozen entropy schedules already covered by the PBT contract;
  • causal cost stages 0 -> 0.25 -> 0.5 -> 1 bp at target-owned sampled-environment steps 0 / 48k / 96k / 144k;
  • a five-update PBT/checkpoint cadence, ten-update burn-in, retained-best checkpoints, and exact generation-bound provenance;
  • a matched exact-1-bp arm that may run only after the causal-cost arm is reviewed.

The masked, position-only, and time-only cells from the completed December 2025 4x3x40 replication remain the attribution reference. A winning combined-state recipe must later be frozen and replayed against those arms; spending four independent PBT populations on the four state masks would confound state attribution with search luck.

New chronological selection block

The PBT search does not select again on the December 2025 attribution panel. The predeclared January 2026 panel is dataset_v2_r4_actor_jan2026_crossed_8x4_20260929.json. It contains eight outcome-uninspected RTH anchors from January 2, 5, and 6, crossed with the frozen 96/128/160/200 horizons. All 32 tasks were validated against the symbol-safe validation partition before launch. It is a development selection block, not the sealed final test.

Gates and bounded accounting

The 96k-step review is the continuation gate. Mechanical continuation requires all four members, finite/non-empty reports, at least two genuine exploit attempts after burn-in, exact target receipts, monotonic target-owned clocks, and no stale or pending lineage. The host must retain at least 2 GiB free RAM.

Economic promotion remains stricter than PBT donor selection:

  1. positive 1 bp net return and positive Sharpe per square-root bar;
  2. no effectively all-flat policy (flat < 98%) and explicit turnover reporting;
  3. sustained passing reports rather than one transient point;
  4. exact fresh-process checkpoint replay;
  5. comparison with flat, long-only, short-only, random, and untrained controls;
  6. paired chronological-block uncertainty and 0.5/1/2 bp replay;
  7. a frozen masked/position/time/both replication and an independent population seed before economic promotion.

Sharpe / sqrt(bar) >= 0.02 and a +0.005 margin over the strongest directional control remain high-signal challenger targets, not substitutes for the full promotion gate.

This is a valid PBT search result but not solver promotion. The identical January-panel controls were flat 0%, long-only -0.481611%, short-only +0.441611%, random -1.385487%, and untrained -0.127701%. The learned population therefore tied the strongest directional control exactly; its apparent edge is the panel's short direction, not demonstrated conditional trading.

PBT itself did search. Six exploit decisions converged the population onto the best available short lineage while lower learning rates and entropy schedules continued to mutate. The causal cost stages were delivered at target-owned 0 / 48k / 96k / 144k steps, and all members continued after the final exact-1-bp transition. This keeps causal cost staging as a mechanically valid curriculum factor, but does not establish economic uplift.

Across members, positive-net reports numbered 20 / 25 / 29 / 15; the first occurred at approximately 103k / 78k / 58k / 128k target-owned steps. The strongest recorded report was member 00001 at 107,056 steps: +0.444556% net and 0.024954 Sharpe with action fractions 99.9430% short / 0.0244% flat / 0.0326% long. That apparent increment over short-only was not checkpoint-stable and was far below the declared +0.005 Sharpe margin.

The only positive report in the entire exact-cost population occurred in member 00001 at 9,776 steps: +0.003367% net and 0.002014 Sharpe while 99.9844% flat. It failed the explicit near-flat gate and never recurred. PBT continued to perturb learning rate, entropy schedule, discount, GAE lambda, minibatch size, SGD passes, clipping, and value-loss settings; it did not recover a non-flat policy. The result therefore separates a valid search from an economic failure: exact 1 bp from update zero makes inactivity the stable basin for this search space.

Because every trained exact-cost report was ineligible, its retained forensic best pointers refer to update-zero checkpoints. All four replayed exactly but remained economically negative. The four terminal checkpoints also replayed exactly at the all-flat zero result. There is no hidden trained winner behind checkpoint selection.

Retained-best replay

Three of four retained-best checkpoints reproduced their selected score exactly in fresh processes. Member 00001 was rejected by the strict replay gate. Its recorded best report was almost short-only and contained one flat and one long action; native RLlib and manual replay consistently changed the flat action to short while preserving the long action. The delta was 7.4030326e-06 in search score (0.0007403 percentage points of net return).

This is not evidence of meaningful edge. It is evidence that a decision near an action-logit tie was not checkpoint-stable. Exact parity remains the gate; future candidate reports must include the minimum top-two action-logit margin so restore-sensitive ties are visible before promotion.

Harness defect found by controls

The first control-panel invocation completed the flat evaluation but the outer collector rejected its artifact. Baseline runs namespace stable metrics by experiment, while the outer collector only understood the legacy flat stable directory. A red regression reproduced the mismatch and a narrow compatibility repair accepts the experiment-scoped layout while retaining the legacy fallback. The repaired control panel then completed all five arms. This reporting-only patch is kept outside the source-matched PBT pair until the exact-1-bp comparator closes.

The failed run is retained as provenance and cannot contribute economic evidence. The repair is narrow: ITraderEnv still owns mutable training task overrides, the evaluation task panel remains unchanged, and no solver/reward/task semantics changed.

After the matched PBT pair closed, the tested collector repair was restored to the working tree. It now resolves experiment-scoped baseline metrics first and retains the legacy flat-layout fallback. The source fingerprints of the two PBT arms therefore remain identical.

Controls, uncertainty, and cost-sensitivity decision

The causal arm's terminal action receipt is exactly the short-only control, episode for episode; its paired chronological-block delta is therefore identically zero. The exact-cost terminal receipt is exactly flat, so its paired delta to the flat control is also identically zero. These are stronger rejection statements than overlapping confidence intervals.

No checkpoint survives all of non-flatness, directional-control uplift, and exact replay. The predeclared 0.5/1/2 bp replay and independent-population replication are therefore not spent on a non-candidate. They remain mandatory for the first future checkpoint that clears those earlier gates; omitting them here is a bounded-search stop, not a waived promotion requirement.

Final decision and next boundary

This goal closes without an economic winner. PBT, sampled-step scheduling, causal reward delivery, retention, lineage, replay, and aggregate reporting are now mechanically valid. The negative result is model/search behavior: the causal arm finds the January panel's short-only direction, while exact cost from update zero finds flat.

Preserve R0 unchanged for historical parity and preserve R0 + R4-v1 as the architecture-qualified feed-forward baseline. Do not claim that R4-v1 passed the economic gate, that the market is fully observed, or that causal staging created conditional alpha. After review of this dirty tree, the next representation boundary is the predeclared clean R1 versus R1 + R4-v1 comparison, with a matched Ray-2.58 R0 control and the same attribution rules. R4-v2 remains deferred.
