Pilot A: episode direction, timing, and cost

Exact estimand

For the exact H decision steps already used by EpisodeEvidence, let p_k be the signed target position applied for step k, and let r_k be that step's recorded simple close-to-close asset return. Do not reconstruct either series from a different index convention.

G            = sum_(k=0..H-1) p_k * r_k
p_bar        = (1 / H) * sum_(k=0..H-1) p_k
L            = sum_(k=0..H-1) r_k
directional  = p_bar * L
timing       = G - directional
C_actual     = c * (sum_(k=0..H-1) |p_k - p_(k-1)| + |p_(H-1)|)
net          = directional + timing - C_actual

Here p_(-1) is the episode's recorded starting position (zero in the current reset contract) and c = evidence_cost_bps * 1e-4. C_actual includes the one real terminal liquidation already included by EpisodeEvidence; it must not be added twice.

This is an additive simple-return identity, matching the current ITraderEnv evidence arithmetic. It is not a compounded wealth decomposition. timing is the observed-horizon residual relative to constant signed exposure p_bar. It is not clipped and is not automatically alpha, forecasting skill, causality, or a market-timing estimate.

The identity cancels exactly: directional + timing = G, so attribution cannot change net. For example, if G = 30 bps, directional = 20 bps, timing = 10 bps, and cost is 4 bps, net remains 26 bps. L is the unselected always-long gross return on the same bars; it is not a hindsight "best direction," and neither L nor directional is clamped. Signed negative exposure works unchanged. A constant long or short path has zero timing residual because G = p_bar * L; flat from zero does too. None of this is a capability, alpha, or causal-skill claim.

Compute the fields per complete episode, then apply the unchanged Q24 order: four horizons within anchor, anchors within symbol, and twelve symbols equally. Q25 continues to normalize each episode before aggregation. A pooled step mean or the raw 128-task mean may be secondary only; neither replaces Q24.

Required reconciliation tests

  • Reconcile G exactly with stored gross return and reconcile both net = G - C_actual and net = directional + timing - C_actual within one declared numerical tolerance.
  • Cover every prior/target position transition and charge exactly one actual terminal liquidation.
  • Make constant long and constant short have zero timing residual; make an all-flat, zero-start episode have zero gross, direction, timing, and cost.
  • Include a path where additive and compounded calculations differ, and assert that this pilot follows the additive result.
  • Reject missing, duplicated, censored, mismatched-step, non-finite, or wrong-cost traces. Never repair them by clipping or partial aggregation.
  • Prove row-order invariance and preserve the accepted Q24/Q25 reducers.

Pilot B: causal one-step realized-return diagnostic

Freeze first, score second

For a decision at source row i, persist the decision identity, policy action, prior position, and the 60 visible close levels from rows i-60 through i-1 before row i is exposed. Those levels produce exactly 59 trailing simple returns. The subsequently attached outcome is the return from row i-1 to row i; it is not part of the scale window.

The following scale is a recommended candidate for the next GRILL, not an adopted estimator. It is deliberately narrow and versioned:

scale_id = lagged_close_std_60c_59r_ddof1_v0
u_j      = (C_j - C_(j-1)) / max(C_(j-1), 1e-8), j=i-59,...,i-1
sigma_i  = sqrt(sum_j (u_j - mean(u))^2 / 58)

Its unit is dimensionless one-minute return. If the candidate is approved, use the same sigma_i for the policy and every reference action at that decision. The proposed invalid-scale rule is: if the scale is non-finite or sigma_i <= 1e-12, keep all raw fields, set normalized fields explicitly unavailable, record the reason, and increment coverage counters. The estimator and cutoff both require human approval. The cutoff is a proposed numerical-degeneracy guard aligned with current evidence code, not a significance threshold or a literature-derived permanent choice.

Only after the frozen record is durable may the next simple asset return r_next be attached. For prior position q, candidate target position a in {-1, 0, +1}, and cost rate c:

gross(a)           = a * r_next
switch_cost(a)     = c * |a - q|
continuation_net(a)= gross(a) - switch_cost(a)
hypothetical_roundtrip_net_return(a) = continuation_net(a) - c * |a|
volatility_scaled_continuation_net_return(a)
                       = continuation_net(a) / sigma_i  # only when valid

continuation_net is the primary one-step diagnostic. hypothetical_roundtrip_net_return is a separately named hypothetical forced close. Neither may alter the episode's actual terminal liquidation or EpisodeEvidence.

At every eligible decision, score the policy action and the named references hold_prior, flat, long, and short with the same q, outcome, cost, and scale. Preserve semantic labels even when two labels resolve to the same action. A random draw adds no action coverage because all three actions are already enumerated; the seeded-random terminal control remains unchanged. max_a continuation_net(a) may be stored only as oracle_diagnostic_only and must never be described as attainable policy PnL.

No output from this pilot may be named Sharpe. A single outcome does not supply a return series or dispersion estimate; the primary-source rationale remains in the capability diagnostics evidence and Sharpe's differential-return definition.

Candidate pilot output and tests

The opt-in sidecar should retain raw and normalized action vectors, scale value and validity, selected action, references, cost convention, observation/trace identity, and an explicit diagnostic-only/oracle marker. If summarized, first form an episode-level result over common valid steps and record valid count and coverage; only then may the existing Q24 hierarchy be applied. Do not pool all steps into a new headline.

Minimum tests:

  • 60 close levels produce 59 returns and sample standard deviation uses denominator 58 (ddof=1), verified against a hand calculation.
  • Perturbing any future close cannot change the frozen action, prior position, trailing returns, or scale; it may change only the attached outcome fields.
  • Exhaust all nine prior-position/target-action transition costs.
  • Policy and references share one outcome and one scale at each decision.
  • Zero, NaN, and infinite scales make normalized values unavailable while raw values survive; no epsilon substitution is allowed.
  • Actual episode liquidation and hypothetical roundtrip liquidation remain separately identifiable and cannot be double-counted.
  • Default-off execution produces no sidecar and no training/evaluation behavior change. Static checks should prevent any diagnostic field from entering rewards, observations, TaskSpec, proposer feedback, or checkpoint choice.

Recommended module seams for DEV

These are interface recommendations, not adopted class names or implementation authority. DEV may preserve the approved default-off pilot shell while the estimator and reporting subdecisions remain unconfigured.

  1. A small frozen-decision trace interface owns causal serialization and outcome attachment. Its invariant is that decision-side fields exist before an outcome can be attached.
  2. A pure episode-attribution evaluator owns the additive identities, validation, and reconciliation. Callers should not duplicate formulas.
  3. A pure one-step evaluator owns the lagged-scale version, all three target actions, reference labels, invalid-scale policy, and oracle marking.
  4. An opt-in diagnostic reporter owns sidecar persistence and later aggregation. Prefer this to widening EpisodeEvidence before the pilot earns stability.

Each output needs a schema version and joins to dataset, source, config, checkpoint, task, symbol/session/anchor, horizon, cost, policy/control, seed, producer, and method identities. The modules should expose compact validated records and hide their arithmetic and failure policy. This keeps the evaluation seam deep without leaking experimental fields into the solver or online shell.

Those facts motivate the separate-data route; they do not select dates or certify a new panel.

The new snapshot must have its own immutable dataset identity and the approved first-class terminal_screen_only role with certified eligible intervals and separate training/terminal dataset identities. Code and independent review are pending. The snapshot is not appended to dataset v2, passed through a new 70/15/15 split, used for training, proposer/curriculum generation, tuning, calibration, checkpoint selection, or treated as a replacement final test. The existing training/validation source stays unchanged, and the existing final test stays sealed.

Reuse the existing HistoricalMarketDataSource, AlpacaMarketDataSource, and immutable snapshot builder. They already provide a read-only acquisition adapter, split-adjusted one-minute SIP requests, raw page/request retention, retrieval timestamps, UTC/session normalization, duplicate rejection, stable ordering, hashes, and no forward fill. Do not create a second provider backend for this route.

The data layer is reusable, but the current evaluation path cannot yet express this route. run_terminal_screen requires the terminal dataset hash to equal the completed training screen's dataset hash; panel_qualification requires the candidate hash to equal the retained exposure inventory's hash and assumes that inventory is non-empty. The qualifier also assumes data_split=validation, 0.7/0.15/0.15, and a purge boundary. terminal_panel_report also rejects episode rows not labeled validation; DataPartition/ITraderEnv accept only the existing split vocabulary, EpisodeEvidence copies that split, and the experimental diagnostic runner currently admits only train/validation.

The approved role therefore needs an end-to-end bridge, not just a preflight flag. Keep terminal_screen_only as a first-class role bound to the terminal dataset identity and certified-interval hash; do not alias it to validation. Certified intervals replace fractional splitting for eligibility, while the existing DataPartition continuity/session checks remain reusable. Environment reset/evidence, diagnostic collection, qualification, preflight, and terminal reporting must all preserve and validate the role plus certificate. DEV has been dispatched for scoped TDD and independent review. Every consumer must fail closed until that seam lands; relabeling the new snapshot as validation would misstate provenance.

  • provider/feed, adjustment, timeframe, timezone, exchange-calendar identity, session policy, missing-bar/no-forward-fill policy, retrieval time, raw-page hashes, manifest hash, and usage classification;
  • the same twelve symbols, unless a separately approved scientific question explicitly changes the universe;
  • enough continuous same-session support for a 60-bar past window and each 96/128/160/200 future horizon at the declared 60/120-minute decision offsets;
  • enough metadata support for 32 distinct symbol/session anchors, at least two per symbol, before crossing the four horizons;
  • timestamp/session duplication, OHLC-schema presence, gap, early-close, and complete-final-bar checks without reading price values;
  • calendar-component counts and breadth needed to evaluate Q26 candidates, without choosing a Q26 method; and
  • an exposure registry covering training, curriculum/proposer generation, tuning, calibration, progress/terminal panels, diagnostics, plots, human inspection, and agent/tool access. Any uncertain interval is excluded.

Selection must be outcome-blind. Do not choose a provider, period, date, anchor, or exclusion by return, volatility, regime, policy action, or solver result. Date novelty alone is not non-exposure proof. The complete feasibility contract is in the Q23 evidence note.

Q26 and future autoresearch

Q26 method and numerical choices remain entirely unresolved: calendar grouping, resampling method, block length or grid, null calibration/coverage tolerance, interval construction, multiplicity family, failure policy, and the interpretation of two training seeds still require outcome-blind analysis and human approval. The approved research sequence first freezes the outcome-blind layout, then measures Type-I error, interval coverage, numerical/support failure, and stability on predeclared synthetic null dependence families. Separately named simulated-effect power cannot rescue a method that fails null calibration. Every failed null and method attempt remains recorded. Neither pilot may open the terminal panel early or expand the confirmatory family.

Only after Q27 review closure, the two pilot contracts pass their remaining tests, the Q23 snapshot/panel is metadata-sealed, and Q26 is accepted may the autoresearch-optimizer loop be considered. One eligible first objective is:

minimize median wall-clock seconds to produce both validated diagnostic
sidecars on one frozen non-terminal fixture

The baseline and challenger must consume identical bytes and emit semantically identical validated records; the holdout stays frozen, one implementation factor changes per challenge, and tests plus a fixed memory ceiling are hard gates. This objective optimizes mechanics only. It may not change data, dates, rewards, economic thresholds, Q24/Q25, Q26, pilot formulas, or any market outcome.
