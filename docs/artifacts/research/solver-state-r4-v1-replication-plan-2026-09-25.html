What has and has not become Markov

For the present exact-cost, finite-horizon net_return task, current position determines the cost of a new target action and remaining horizon determines the termination/liquidation boundary. Exposing both removes the known endogenous control-state alias. The exogenous market process remains partially observed; therefore the correct claim is control-state completion for this task contract, not that trading as a whole is a fully observed MDP.

ITraderEnv owns both values and observation assembly. TaskSpec remains the problem/curriculum contract, RewardEngine remains the reward seam, and neither the proposer nor deterministic risk/execution shells acquire mutable portfolio state.

R4-v1.1 initialization correction

The completed R4-v1 run used ordinary random initialization for its residual heads. Although masked [0, 0] observations were inert, nonzero state arms did not begin from the same action preferences as R0. The new solver_state_residual_init=zero_v1 profile zero-initializes only the final actor and value residual heads. The base market trunk, task FiLM path, state encoder, parameter count, and observation contract remain unchanged.

The update-zero gate requires matching base weights, action argmax parity on nonzero state inputs, logits and values equal within 1e-7, zero residual-head weights, and arm-matched untrained replays. Historical checkpoints continue to restore with the compatibility default random_v1.

What improved

The combined state arm improved terminal net return over masked in every paired seed: +0.1952, +0.0689, and +0.0014 percentage points. Its pooled seed-then-anchor mean effect was +0.0885 percentage points with a 95% interval of [+0.0037, +0.1889], so the net-return factor gate passed. Its mean turnover fell from 11.40 to 4.71. Position-only also improved net in every seed, while time-only produced the strongest mean gross return and critic fit.

This is not merely cost avoidance. Mean gross return moved from -0.0104% for masked to +0.0064% for position, +0.0196% for time, and +0.0112% for both. However, the paired Sharpe effect was not seed-consistent and its pooled interval crossed zero.

What failed

Seed 20260926 converged to a near-flat basin: terminal flat fractions were 100.00%, 99.77%, 99.97%, and 99.39% for masked, position, time, and both. The explicit <0.98 ceiling correctly rejects all four arms. Seed 20260924 remained economically negative, and masked also exceeded the turnover ceiling at 29.25. Updates 38–40 do not rescue the verdict; the combined arm's final-three mean is negative for seeds 20260924 and 20260926.

The useful transient is seed 20260925. Combined state retained +0.0672% mean net over updates 38–40, but terminal Sharpe remained slightly negative. This is a real learned checkpoint, not provenance contamination, yet it is insufficient for promotion.

Actor and critic diagnostics

Masked-state counterfactuals are exactly inert. In active position-only arms, flipping position changed the greedy action on 18.21%, 6.06%, and 0.11% of steps across the three seeds. In time-only arms, mirroring remaining horizon changed far fewer greedy actions, but changed the value estimate by a mean absolute 0.959, 0.578, and 0.358. Combined arms used both coordinates in the non-collapsed seeds. These results show that the state branch is learned and that episode phase materially improves the critic, while actor use remains seed-sensitive and disappears in the near-flat basin.

Interventions on coordinates disabled for an arm are out of distribution and are excluded from interpretation. PPO logits are not Q-values; the diagnostics demonstrate state sensitivity, not a decomposed cost-aware action-value model.

Final gate decision

The combined position-plus-time connector passes architecture qualification and the relative paired net-return factor gate. It fails the absolute economic gate. Accordingly:

  1. preserve state-free R0 as the immutable parity control;
  2. use R0 + R4-v1 as the forward Phase A feed-forward training baseline;
  3. do not claim economic promotion, a profitable solver, or a fully observed market MDP;
  4. retain position + remaining_horizon_fraction together even though their effects differ, because both are required by the exact-cost finite-horizon transition/reward contract;
  5. keep episode PnL, accounting, holding-age, turnover-budget, drawdown, and market-session features in the semantics registry until their corresponding transition, reward, or risk semantics exist;
  6. make the next bounded solver step an actor-robustness search over the already authorized baseline—learning rate, entropy pressure, and causal cost staging, with retained-best replay and the same flat/turnover guards—before R4-v2;
  7. preserve clean state-free R1 and named R1 + R4-v1 cells when recurrence begins, so recurrence and observability remain attributable.

Authoritative evidence is in analysis.json. The comparison plots include net return, Sharpe, action fractions, and PPO diagnostics.

Decision boundary

Architecture qualification is accepted: future feed-forward Phase A work uses R0 + R4-v1, with R0 retained in every parity-sensitive comparison. Economic promotion is rejected and cannot be inferred from critic fit or relative uplift. Run the bounded actor-robustness search before spending on R4-v2. Carry both R1 and R1 + R4-v1 long enough to attribute recurrence separately.

Deferred R4-v2 design question

The leading research shape is centered nonlinear late fusion after task FiLM:

h_market = MLP(market_window)
h_task = FiLM(h_market, task)
z_state = state_encoder(agent_state)
delta = joint_connector(h_task, z_state) - joint_connector(h_task, zero_state)
logits = policy_head(h_task) + policy_residual(delta)
value = value_head(h_task) + value_residual(delta)

Centering preserves masked-state parity while allowing market/task × state interactions. It remains separate because it changes the function class and the causal interpretation of the residual.
