Frozen question and design

Does the accepted fixed-width R4-v1 state connector improve the frozen R0 MLP-FiLM solver under exact 1 bp economics, and is any relative effect strong and durable enough to pass the separate absolute economic gate?

Predeclared decision gates

These gates were frozen before inspecting any result from this round:

  1. All 12 cells must complete with identical observation schema, dataset, evaluation-panel, and source-tree fingerprints, finite required metrics, and no provenance drift.
  2. Primary attribution uses terminal update 12, paired by training seed against that seed's masked arm. Best-of-trajectory evidence is secondary.
  3. The final three evaluation reports (updates 10–12) must be reported as a durability slice; a single terminal point cannot hide late instability.
  4. Every primary terminal checkpoint must restore and replay exactly in a fresh process. If the current best-checkpoint pointer is nonterminal, terminal replay must use the update-12 checkpoint-index entry explicitly.
  5. Each terminal checkpoint requires diagnostics conditioned by prior position and remaining-time quarter, including switching, turnover, liquidation, inference entropy, explained variance, action probabilities, and value outputs.
  6. Uncertainty is paired by task/episode and clustered or bootstrapped by the eight market anchors. The 32 anchor-horizon tasks are not independent. Intervals are reported per seed and as a pooled/replicated interpretation.
  7. The relative factor gate requires directionally consistent paired uplift and a predeclared 95% anchor-block interval excluding zero for any promoted metric. Net-return and Sharpe effects are computed and reported separately.
  8. The absolute economic gate is separate: positive exact-1-bp net return and Sharpe, superiority to flat and the strongest directional/untrained control, reasonable turnover, and exact replay. Relative uplift alone cannot promote.
  9. No sealed final-test access, 2-bp promotion claim, PBT, or tuning based on intermediate results is allowed.
  10. Cells run sequentially. Free RAM is checked between cells and the official runner fails closed below 2 GiB.

Uncertainty method frozen before results

For each seed and candidate arm, compute episode-level candidate-minus-masked deltas on the identical 32 tasks. Define the cluster as the panel's eight unique market anchors (symbol, start_idx); all four horizons belonging to an anchor travel together. Produce 10,000 seeded nonparametric bootstrap replicates by sampling eight anchors with replacement and averaging all carried task deltas. Report the percentile 95% interval for net return and for per-episode Sharpe.

For the pooled interpretation, preserve replication: first sample the three training seeds with replacement, then independently sample eight anchors with replacement inside each selected seed, and average the resulting deltas. The pooled interval is evidence across the three declared training replications, not 32 independent tasks and not an independent final test.

Controls

Flat, long-only, short-only, seeded-random, and seeded-untrained controls use the same dataset, evaluation seed, exact 1 bp evidence cost, and frozen panel. The strongest control is chosen separately for net return and Sharpe from these predeclared controls; it is not selected from additional post-hoc baselines.

Exact replay and state-conditioned diagnostics

All 12 update-12 checkpoints restored in fresh Ray processes and reproduced the authoritative terminal net score. Replay deltas were exactly zero or at most 2.17e-19.

Each diagnostic contains 4,672 decisions, action probabilities, value outputs, switching, and slices by prior position and remaining-time quarter. Across seeds, mean step-level switching was 6.83% masked, 7.38% position, 3.42% time, and 3.67% both. Time and both reduced turnover and switching while their mean explained variance increased to 0.155 and 0.139 from masked 0.030. Their mean predicted flat probability rose toward termination: time from 46.58% in the early quarter to 48.59% terminal, and both from 44.18% to 46.97%. The full by-position slices show distinct probabilities and value outputs when those states were visited, but several near-flat terminal policies did not visit every prior-position category. These diagnostics establish state use and value-path influence; they do not rescue the failed absolute economics.

Evidence/tooling corrections

Three narrow evidence defects were closed with red tests before use:

  1. the ablation runner now enforces a top-level free-RAM floor before every sequential cell;
  2. replay and state diagnostics now select terminal explicitly rather than silently following a transient best_checkpoint.json pointer;
  3. control stable evidence is namespaced by experiment so seeded random and untrained episode receipts cannot overwrite one another.

The new seed-then-anchor bootstrap also has a regression test proving that the four crossed horizons move together inside each market-anchor cluster. Only the six invalidated random/untrained controls were rerun after the control-path fix.

Verdict

Fail R4-v1 economic promotion. Time-only passes the relative net factor gate, which is useful evidence that remaining-horizon observability changes the learned solution in a favorable direction. It is not the complete Markov state, its three-seed mean net and Sharpe remain negative, and it fails the absolute gate. The complete position-plus-time arm does not pass a pooled relative interval and also fails the absolute gate. Neither becomes the default solver observation, enters PBT, or justifies sealed-test or 2-bp promotion work.

This verdict closes the special R4-before-R1 execution block. It does not authorize an automatic R1 launch: the existing user-review and recurrent lifecycle/placement gates still apply. If R1 proceeds after that review, R4-v1 must remain a named, separate observability factor rather than being silently folded into the recurrent architecture. The next R4 work, if any, should use a genuinely new chronological development block and test the time signal as an explicit factor; rerunning or tuning against these same eight anchors is not allowed.

Post-audit architecture decision

The economic verdict above is unchanged. After reviewing control-state correctness separately from immediate returns, R0 remains the immutable parity control and the complete R0 + R4-v1 connector is architecture-qualified as the forward feed-forward Phase A training baseline. This reflects removal of the known position/termination alias, not economic promotion or full market observability. The follow-up uses a fresh December-2025 block, zero-initialized residual heads, executable turnover/near-flat gates, arm-matched update-zero controls, counterfactual diagnostics, and a fixed 40-update 4x3 replication. R1 must retain state-free and explicitly named R1 + R4-v1 cells.
