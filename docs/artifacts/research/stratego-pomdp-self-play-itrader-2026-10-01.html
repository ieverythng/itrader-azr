Imperfect-information self-play and learning-progress curricula for iTrader#

Date: 2026-10-01

Status: research artifact; design-only; no implementation, architecture promotion, training launch, or final-test access is authorized by this document

Scope: primary-source review of Ataraxos and Self-Play Pretraining with Zero Data, followed by a seam-preserving map into the registered iTrader research wings. The two source mechanisms are intentionally kept separate.

Executive decision#

There are useful ideas here, but the headline is narrower than “turn trading into Stratego self-play.” The existing R-series sequence remains first. Around that fixed sequence, the strongest transfers are:

  1. Zero-data self-play -> proposer curriculum: once one solver recipe is frozen and repeatable, test whether a validated TaskSpec is useful by the learning progress it induces in that solver, then add fresh/replay/mutation and quality-diversity controls. This is the cleanest route toward a real co-adaptive AZR-inspired loop.
  2. Ataraxos -> bounded PPO/evaluation controls: first audit the resolved entropy, KL, clipping, critic, and advantage contracts; then compare raw versus EMA checkpoints. Coordinated schedules and absolute-advantage filtering are candidates, not presumed cures. They remain optimizer/evaluation factors, not new solver architectures.
  3. Ataraxos belief/search -> new PF, WM, and TTS research axes: a causal, calibrated conditional distribution over future market outcomes may be useful, but it is not the same object as Stratego's posterior over currently existing hidden pieces. Test-time search is deferred until an independently validated generative market scenario model exists.

The following are not justified:

  • literal opponent self-play against historical OHLCV;
  • treating the market as a two-player zero-sum game;
  • adding a raw action tape to Family 00 merely because Stratego uses move history;
  • conditioning decisions on realized future bars or future actions;
  • calling a forward-return classifier a POMDP belief state;
  • feeding future-derived regime tags into the solver;
  • redesigning R4-v2 around Ataraxos.

R4-v2's identity is frozen for this review: it is the separately named nonlinear joint-fusion factor already defined by the project. The current workspace still records its implementation/evidence status as deferred, while the user reports it as staged in the active parallel work. That is status drift to reconcile, not permission to reinterpret the design. This report neither changes that status nor proposes a different R4-v2. R4-v1 remains the endogenous portfolio-state factor; it does not make the external market fully observed. Active architecture, system architecture, masterplan

The two mechanisms are not the same kind of self-play#

Calling both systems “self-play” is terminologically valid but architecturally misleading. Ataraxos improves a policy through games against itself. The zero-data paper implements a co-evolving teacher–student curriculum in which one model generates training examples for another.

Formal causality correction#

Let the causal history available at time $t$ be

h_t=(o_{0:t}, a_{0:t-1}, r_{0:t-1}, e_{0:t}),

where $e$ contains any causally available endogenous portfolio/accounting state. A deployable policy is

a_t \sim \pi(\cdot\mid h_t, \tau),

with task contract $\tau$. The current action can depend on causal history and an internal state computed from it. It cannot condition on the realized future bars, rewards, or actions $(o_{t+1:},r_{t:},a_{t+1:})$.

Its value nevertheless integrates possible futures:

Q^{\pi}(h_t,a_t)= \mathbb{E}_{\substack{o_{t+1:},a_{t+1:}\sim P,\pi}} \left[\sum_{k\geq 0}\gamma^k r_{t+k}\mid h_t,a_t\right].

This is the precise sense in which later policy behaviour affects a present decision. The present action depends on a distribution over possible future trajectories, not on the future trajectory that will later be observed.

Three state objects must remain distinct:

  1. POMDP belief: $b_t(z)=P(z_t=z\mid h_t)$, a posterior over a currently latent state under an explicit latent-state model.
  2. World model / predictive distribution: $P(o_{t+1:t+H}\mid h_t,a_{t:t+H-1})$, a distribution over future observations. For the present price-taking simulator, market bars do not become action-conditioned merely because portfolio PnL does.
  3. Endogenous portfolio state: known causal variables such as current position and remaining horizon. R4-v1 exposes two such variables. They are not beliefs and not forecasts.

An exact Bayesian belief can be a Markov information state for a specified POMDP, but Ataraxos does not demonstrate that its learned approximation makes an arbitrary domain exactly Markov. It uses a large engineered information state for the policy and a separate approximate posterior only when performing search.

Ataraxos: what the system actually does#

1. Two policy/value processes trained in tandem#

Stratego has a private setup phase and an alternating-move phase. Ataraxos uses a decoder-only setup transformer and an encoder-only move transformer. It samples setups and moves directly from the corresponding networks during self-play. Setup choices determine the initial boards; completed-game outcomes update both setup and move processes. The authors chose the decomposition because the two phases are substantially different tasks, not because the setup network is a generic open-ended task proposer. Nature Methods

The released move network is an eight-layer, 384-wide transformer. It produces move logits from a query–key product over board-cell embeddings and a categorical win/draw/loss value distribution. It is not recurrent. Move-network source

2. Engineered information state, not learned temporal recurrence#

The paper representation contains 455 current-board channels plus 32 recent-move channels. The current-board block includes own piece types, heuristic opponent type probabilities under uniform setup/move assumptions, hidden/empty/moved flags, progress toward draw limits, and accumulated threat, evade, adjacency, capture, death, protection, and starting-location information. Each recent move is a board plane with (-1) at its source and (+1) at its destination. Supplement, Section S2

In source, 355 board-state planes and the separate piece-ID grid are combined by the feature orchestrator; the move policy selects the latest 32 movement planes. Channel registry, feature orchestration

The article is explicit that Ataraxos accesses history through features rather than learning across time directly. It reports that temporal attention or recurrence helped belief modelling under compute normalization, but did not produce an analogous out-of-the-box compute-normalized policy improvement due to runtime/memory cost and interaction with advantage filtering. This is evidence against using Ataraxos as an argument that iTrader should replace its R-series definitions with a recurrent Stratego clone. Opportunities for improvement

3. Dynamically damped PPO-like learning#

The move objective combines:

  • clipped policy improvement with ratio range ([0.8,1.2]);
  • reverse KL from current policy to the data-collection policy, coefficient (0.1);
  • reverse KL to a “magnet” policy;
  • categorical value cross-entropy;
  • gradient norm clipping and an annealed Adam learning rate.

The Nature paper and supplement report a magnet that first selects a movable piece uniformly and then a legal move for that piece uniformly. It is not a no-op or safety policy. The pinned public checkout differs: its default selects uniformly over legal actions, while piece-first sampling is a non-default branch, and the reviewed release does not evidence a final-run override. Paper-reported and released-code magnets must therefore remain distinct provenance claims. The regularization coefficient and learning rate decay by power laws. The released defaults include (lambda_{return}=0.8), (lambda_{advantage}=0.5), PPO clip (0.2), EMA (0.999), advantage-filter quantile (0.75), magnitude floor (0.01), and one training epoch for moves. Supplement, Tables S6–S7, RL configuration and loss, loss implementation, magnet helper

The absolute-advantage filter retains only positions at or above both the batch's 75th percentile and the fixed 0.01 floor. With non-tied advantages it retains roughly the upper quarter, and the fixed floor can reduce that further; ties can retain more. It is not merely a low-confidence-label filter. Buffer source

For iTrader's current three always-legal actions, a uniform Ataraxos-style magnet is not a new loss:

\mathrm{KL}(\pi\,\|\,U_3)=\log 3-H(\pi).

Minimizing it is entropy maximization up to an additive constant. iTrader already anneals entropy, clips PPO ratios and gradients, and searched multiple entropy schedules. The source-derived novelty worth testing is the coordination of update size, behavior-policy KL, and regularization strength, not relabelling the existing entropy term as a magnet.

4. Separate belief network trained after the final policy#

Ataraxos trains the belief model on every position in trajectories generated by the final self-play setup and move policies. Given the acting player's information state, it teacher-forces an autoregressive prediction of the true opponent hidden piece types. Ground truth exists because the simulator knows the complete board. Dropout is used to improve generalization to policies unlike self-play, including humans. Nature belief modelling

The reported Stratego belief network is a transformer encoder plus autoregressive transformer decoder, not the policy's recurrent memory. Its reported size is 57.1M parameters. The repository also contains temporal and LSTM belief variants, but their presence in source must not be mistaken for the reported final configuration. Belief source, belief loss, Supplement, Tables S8 and S11

5. Test-time search refines only the current decision#

For each move, the reported Stratego search:

  1. samples hidden-piece configurations from the learned posterior;
  2. forces each legal root action under sampled configurations;
  3. performs 1,000 depth-40 rollouts, with the blueprint move policy controlling both players after the forced move;
  4. averages the leaf value predictions into action-value estimates;
  5. takes one tabular magnetic-mirror-descent-like update of the current action distribution, regularized to the magnet and blueprint; and
  6. samples the played move from that updated distribution.

The reported reverse-KL coefficients are 0.002 to the magnet and 0.02 to the blueprint policy. The update changes only the current tabular decision; it does not fine-tune the network. Supplement, Section S3.7, search source, rollouts, policy update

6. Opponent modelling is limited#

Ataraxos does not learn or adapt an explicit model of the current human opponent's policy. It uses its self-play blueprint for both players in rollouts. The belief approximates the hidden-state posterior induced by self-play policy trajectories; dropout is the reported defence against out-of-distribution play. The 20-game human opponent was explicitly told the bot would not adapt. Any proposal for online market-regime adaptation is therefore a new iTrader hypothesis, not a feature reproduced from Ataraxos. Nature search method and evaluation

7. Scale and evidence#

The Stratego RL run used 16 H100s for one week; belief training used 4 H100s for four days. The paper reports 163M completed games, 208B environment steps, 8.56M move-network gradient steps, and 99.5K setup-network gradient steps. Each GPU ran 1,536 environments and generated 202 moves between updates. Training information, Supplement, Table S3

The headline human result is 15 wins, 1 loss, and 4 draws against Pim Niemeijer; the World Championship demo produced 38 wins and 2 losses. The paper properly notes that human games across the series were not independent and identically distributed, so its binomial calculation is conditional rather than a clean iid test. Cross-game evidence covers Barrage Stratego, Hanabi, and dou dizhu, which is strong evidence that the general design pattern is not confined to one two-player zero-sum game. It is not evidence of trading profitability.

The ablations are informative but conditional on the final configuration. The paper states that remaining hyperparameters were not retuned for each ablated system. Three-seed comparisons find material effects from learning-rate annealing, regularization annealing, and advantage filtering; EMA has similar or better mean performance with lower cross-seed variation. Those results justify isolated iTrader ablations, not wholesale hyperparameter copying. Ablation caveat and EMA result

Self-Play Pretraining with Zero Data: what it actually does#

1. Co-evolving program generator and byte learner#

Both networks are independently parameterized Llama-style decoder transformers initialized from scratch. Each round:

  1. the generator samples programs in an augmented Brainf*ck-like, Turing-complete language;
  2. a bounded universal machine executes each program with a random input tape;
  3. the learner takes a next-byte cross-entropy step on the outputs; and
  4. the generator takes an RL step toward programs aligned with the learner's learning trajectory, plus reward-weighted supervised distillation.

This is not a game-state POMDP, belief-state learner, or future-action planner. Its long horizon is a horizon over learner updates and accumulated curricula. Algorithm

2. Exact learning-progress reward#

At round $e$, the paper defines

p(e)=\lfloor e/2\rfloor,\qquad \Delta\theta_e=\theta_{p(e)}-\theta_e,

and rewards candidate output $y_i$ by

r_i=\left| \left\langle \nabla_\theta\mathcal L(y_i;\theta_e), P_e\odot\Delta\theta_e \right\rangle \right|, \qquad P_e=\frac{\mathrm{lr}}{\sqrt{\hat v_e}+\epsilon}.

It is an absolute, AdamW-preconditioned gradient-alignment score. Forward-mode automatic differentiation avoids materializing a full gradient for every candidate. The intent is to reject both already-mastered examples, which have small gradients, and hard-but-unlearnable noise, whose gradients do not align with demonstrated learner progress. Reward and implementation description

The growing half-history lookback outperformed one-step alignment and realized one-step loss delta in the paper's 1M-parameter, four-seed ablation. Shuffling scores across programs and negating the reward also degraded results. The signed form was slightly better on some individual datasets and worse on most, so the absolute value is supported within this study but is not a universal rule for a PPO trading solver. Reward ablations

3. Generator optimization and curriculum memory#

The generator uses KL-regularized expected reward against a fixed short-program prior, batch-normalized GRPO-style advantages, and sequence-level importance ratios for off-policy replay. It also uses reward-weighted supervised fine-tuning of positively rewarded programs. Generator objective

Its pool combines:

  • fresh generator samples;
  • local single-token mutations of positively rewarded programs; and
  • replayed programs from previous rounds, re-executed with fresh random tapes.

A MAP-Elites-style archive partitions programs by length and dynamic loop depth, retaining multiple strong examples per niche rather than only one global elite. Stored reward decays because usefulness changes as the learner changes. Pool construction

4. Evidence and limits#

The adaptive generator scales more favourably than a fixed prior over the same program space. It discovers arithmetic, Fibonacci, geometric, quadratic, and cubic sequence families much earlier than the reported uniform sampling control, and its learners exhibit in-context behaviour on six held-out synthetic tasks. For each endpoint T, the fixed-corpus control mixes data from 16 generator snapshots spaced through training up to T, then trains a fresh learner. Later endpoint-indexed snapshot mixtures train fresh learners more effectively, which is important evidence that improvement is in the curriculum rather than only in a co-adapted learner. Empirical results

The paper's domain-specific PCFG baseline is stronger on text and code, while the adaptive universal generator transfers more broadly across several non-language modalities. That is direct evidence against replacing iTrader's typed market task grammar with a universal program language merely because the latter is more general.

The released model ladder spans about 98K to 24.4M parameters, uses 4,096-token contexts, and runs to 8,192 self-play rounds. The paper's maximum budget is 34.36B learner tokens. Its published “effective compute” axis counts learner parameters, 1,536 programs per round, context length, rounds, and ensemble seeds; it does not fully price generator optimization, execution, reward JVPs, or hyperparameter search. Hardware and wall-clock cost are not specified well enough to reconstruct total end-to-end expense. Scaling recipe, official scaling code

Natural data supplies no gradient updates during self-play, but DCLM text and DNA validation loss are used to select hyperparameters. The “zero data” experiment therefore has limited evaluation-driven leakage at the research-procedure level. In the later pre-pretraining experiment, the clearest demonstrated benefit is faster natural-data learning; the terminal loss gap narrows and the comparison does not charge the initial self-play compute. Scaling recipe and pre-pretraining

5. It cannot rediscover all pretraining facts#

The paper explicitly says universal pretraining cannot recover contingent information: facts about a particular world must enter through interaction with that world. A universal program space may expose copying, recursion, hierarchy, arithmetic, and other reusable structure. It cannot identify which possible world contains a particular earnings release, law, news event, or historical price path. Longer self-play does not solve this identifiability problem. Discussion

For iTrader, real, chronological OHLCV remains indispensable contingent evidence. The proposer may change which validated tasks are sampled; it must not invent market outcomes and present them as historical evidence.

iTrader system facts that constrain the transfer#

  • TaskSpec is the canonical offline task/problem contract.
  • ITraderEnv owns causal market-window assembly, task-conditioned reset, target-position transitions, and reward context.
  • RewardEngine owns finite solver reward components.
  • Discrete(3) means target positions short, flat, and long. It is not a Stratego move set and flat is not the online WAIT decision.
  • Family 00 is the only enabled learned family. 01/02 are bootstrap contracts, 03 is trade management, and 04 is abstention/unsuitable regime.
  • Historical replay is presently price-taking: solver actions change position, costs, and PnL, not the recorded market path.
  • R4-v1 supplies known endogenous position and remaining-horizon state. It does not infer latent market causes.
  • The online authority chain remains MarketFramer -> CandidateTask(TaskSpec + optional ExecutionIntent) + WAIT -> solver -> DecisionGate -> RiskEngine -> Executor -> DecisionRecord; a CandidateTask may carry a validated optional ExecutionIntent, while broker and execution semantics stay in the online contracts and never in TaskSpec.
  • train_AZR.py currently creates a validated task pool and hands it to PPO once; it is not a co-adaptive proposer/solver loop.

The latest solver evidence also limits what an optimizer transplant may claim. Exact 1 bp from update zero converged to all-flat, while causal cost staging converged to the January panel's short-only control. At the informative 77,832- step checkpoint, greedy replay was 99.358% short even though mean action probabilities were broad (40.18% / 27.30% / 32.52%) and categorical entropy was 1.08234 versus the three-action maximum of 1.09861. Mean explained variance rose only to 0.0376, and clip_fraction is missing/NaN in all 320 captured reports. This is not evidence that “more entropy” is the missing ingredient. It is a reason to repair the critic/clip/advantage diagnostics before advantage filtering or a coordinated damping claim. Current causal forensics

Current source adds six implementation prerequisites:

  • train_solo_V2.py still selects solver_mlp_film; R1–R3 remain design cells, not executable recurrent challengers.
  • the registered legacy LSTM consumes the unsplit full observation and returns its incoming recurrent state unchanged; batch_mode="complete_episodes" does not by itself supply correct BPTT, masking, or state lifecycle;
  • frozen-checkpoint evaluation calls compute_single_action without owning and passing recurrent state, so R2 evidence would be invalid until replay becomes state-aware;
  • each environment currently owns a process-local task pool, and weighted sampling with batch_size=1 reduces to random because elite/recent counts floor to zero; and
  • episode realization mutates seed/start before callback hashing, while feedback remains in-memory, so a stable proposal UID and centralized versioned evidence are prerequisites for curriculum learning; and
  • the current proposer retry counter advances only after a returned task, so repeated backend exceptions require a bounded-failure contract before any co-adaptive loop.

These are seam blockers, not arguments for changing R4-v2.

These boundaries come from the active architecture, system architecture, and masterplan.

Why Stratego is not a direct market analogue#

Stratego property Current iTrader property Consequence
Two strategic agents alter one shared state Historical prices are exogenous to the solver No literal market-opponent self-play
Hidden piece identities exist now and are known to the simulator “Regime” is a chosen latent abstraction; future returns are not a hidden label existing in the simulator A return classifier is predictive, not the same supervised posterior
Actions reveal opponent information and change the board Past target positions add no exogenous market information, but can determine omitted endogenous accounting variables Prefer explicit sufficient accounting state; do not copy a 32-action history block by analogy
Exact rules provide counterfactual rollouts Only one historical future is recorded; a learned scenario model would be approximate Replaying the realized future is an offline counterfactual diagnostic, not online search
Zero-sum terminal win/draw/loss Dense, cost-aware, continuous trading reward Categorical game value and equilibrium claims do not transfer directly
Policy rollouts control both players Solver cannot control future bars Blueprint rollouts require an independently validated exogenous world model
Setup policy changes the initial game distribution Proposer can select tasks/windows but cannot change history Map only to curriculum selection, never synthetic historical truth

The current Family 00 environment can evaluate all three target positions on the same past future path for diagnostics or teacher construction because the market path is exogenous. It cannot expose that path to the deployed policy or call the resulting hindsight choice an online search result.

Recommended separately named ablations#

A. ATX-EMA-EVAL-v0 — lowest-risk first transfer#

Maintain an EMA shadow of solver parameters and evaluate raw and EMA policies at identical checkpoint steps on the identical held-out panel. Training continues from the raw actor; EMA is not silently restored into PBT or treated as another training seed.

Question: does EMA reduce cross-seed/checkpoint variance or improve replay-valid economic metrics without hiding action collapse?

Required evidence: exact raw/EMA checkpoint lineage, net return, Sharpe, turnover, short/flat/long frequencies, logit margins, block uncertainty, and fresh-process replay. This can often be added with no additional sampled environment steps, only evaluation and storage cost.

B. ATX-ADV-FILTER-v0 — promising but high-risk#

This must follow the critic/advantage telemetry repair. Mean explained variance near 0.038 makes top-|advantage| selection capable of selecting critic error or volatility outliers rather than informative transitions. Compare three arms: all valid PPO samples, a top-|advantage| subset, and an equal-retained-count random subset. Do not combine the filter with a new LR schedule, recurrence, S-series head, or proposer.

Log at minimum:

  • raw versus retained samples;
  • threshold and retained fraction by action, position, horizon, symbol, and chronological block, volatility, reward scale, and cost stage;
  • positive/negative advantage balance;
  • clip fraction, KL, entropy, gradient norm, critic loss, and policy/value drift;
  • net, Sharpe, turnover, and action collapse.

The central trading risk is selection bias: rare profitable or risk-reducing events can be discarded if their estimated advantages are noisy or small. Report both equal sampled-environment-step comparisons (sample efficiency) and equal retained-gradient-example or optimizer-FLOP/wall-clock comparisons (compute efficiency). Normalize or stratify thresholds across tasks rather than letting reward scale decide which task contributes updates.

C. ATX-DAMP-SCHED-v0 — low-prior coordinated schedule test#

First produce an exact formula/config receipt. iTrader already anneals entropy, clips PPO ratios and gradients, and PBT searched multiple entropy schedules. Its causal arm retained high stochastic entropy while greedy actions became short-only, so dynamic damping is an unproven factor rather than the obvious cure.

Under a fixed solver and with PBT off, screen learning-rate schedule only and regularization schedule only. Test a predeclared coupled schedule only if the isolated results justify it. Record the direction/reference of every KL term.

Do not create a uniform-magnet arm: with three always-legal actions it is exactly the entropy loss up to a constant. A non-uniform, state-dependent stay or flat prior is a new iTrader objective. “Stay” depends on current position and is distinct from “flat”; either prior would require its own separately justified experiment and may change switching/turnover.

D. PF-PRED-DIST-v0 — predictive state, not a Stratego belief clone#

Keep C0, C1, and shuffled-C1 unchanged. On a separate PF axis, train a causal conditional distribution over exogenous future-bar quantities such as returns, realized volatility, and liquidity/spread proxies. Inputs use only $h_t$; targets may use later bars on the training partition because they are labels, never observation features.

Action- or policy-dependent net PnL and drawdown belong to a separate outcome- model contract conditioned on current accounting state, candidate action, and a fixed subsequent policy/action sequence. They are not labels determined by $h_t$ alone.

Evaluate continuous targets with CRPS/quantile coverage and categorical targets with chronological NLL/Brier score, plus reliability and sharpness against climatology and simple causal baselines. Freeze the solver for the first test. Call this a predictive distribution unless an explicit latent state and transition/observation model justify the term “belief.” Cross it later as accepted solver × PF1; here PF0 means the matched solver without predictive features and PF1 means that solver with the accepted frozen predictive distribution. Do not rename C-series or R4-v2.

E. AZR-LP-DIRECT-v0 — repeated direct learning-progress reference#

For each validated candidate TaskSpec:

  1. clone a fixed solver checkpoint and optimizer state;
  2. perform one small, equal-compute update on candidate-specific causal data;
  3. evaluate pre/post change on an independent chronological capability panel;
  4. record cost-aware uplift and adverse changes; and
  5. discard the clone.

This expensive micro-update is a noisy local intervention/reference estimate of whether the task helped that solver checkpoint, not oracle truth. Repeat it across rollout seeds and report confidence intervals. The record key must include task hash, stable proposal UID, solver and behavior-policy checkpoint digests, optimizer profile, rollout-batch digest, code/data partition hashes, seed, and panel identity because task utility is nonstationary.

F. AZR-LP-GRAD-v0 — validate, do not assume, gradient alignment#

Only after the repeated direct reference is stable, test whether cheap task scores predict it:

  • signed and absolute optimizer-preconditioned gradient alignment;
  • task loss/difficulty;
  • current return or solve rate;
  • gradient norm and gradient sketches;
  • random and score-shuffled controls.

PPO makes the language-model formula non-portable: policy gradients depend on stochastic rollouts and estimated advantages; clipping changes the local objective; value, entropy, and auxiliary losses point in different directions; and task-dependent reward scale can dominate norms. Pin the behavior policy and rollout batch, then score policy-head, value-head, and shared-trunk gradients separately before considering a combined score. Absolute alignment can reward movement in an economically harmful direction. Use repeated held-out economic uplift as the reference before closing the proposer loop.

G. AZR-QD-REPLAY-v0 — typed fresh/mutate/replay curriculum#

Use a typed TaskSpec grammar, deterministic validation, and a quality-diversity archive. Potential archive descriptors include family, horizon, cost band, causal regime-summary bucket, symbol group, and difficulty/uplift band. Mutation changes only permitted typed fields and must never invent an OHLCV path.

Run a Fixed-QD control first: the same fresh/replay/mutation machinery without adaptive utility scores. This isolates diversity and replay from learning-progress selection. For each proposer endpoint, build a fixed corpus by mixing pinned snapshots spaced through proposer training up to that endpoint, then train fresh, identically initialized solvers. This mirrors the paper's strongest co-adaptation control more closely than sampling only the latest proposer.

H. ATX-TTS-DIAG-v0 — defer test-time search#

Ataraxos search needs (a) a posterior or predictive distribution over unresolved state, (b) a fast counterfactual simulator, and (c) a calibrated blueprint value. iTrader already has exact deterministic position/cost/PnL transitions given a price path. It lacks a validated generative future-market transition model, a well-defined supervised belief target, and a blueprint/value calibrated on generated histories. A realized historical future may be used only for an explicit offline hindsight ceiling.

Before any learned-market search is admissible, a scenario model must pass:

  • strict causal input and train-only fitting;
  • proper-scoring-rule improvement over simple baselines;
  • multi-horizon calibration, coverage, tail, and regime-shift checks;
  • independence from the sealed final test;
  • common random scenario seeds across all three root actions;
  • uncertainty propagation through position/cost accounting; and
  • evidence that solver/value rankings remain calibrated under model-generated rather than real histories.

With only three root actions, enumerate all three. Every branch must clone position, remaining horizon, recurrent/PF state, reward normalizer, equity, max-equity, drawdown, turnover, TaskSpec, and reward context rather than mutate a shared state. The Ataraxos root operation is a stateless current-decision logit update, not online neural-weight fine-tuning.

Even then, run search only in offline replay/shadow. The selected target position still passes DecisionGate -> RiskEngine -> Executor; solver flat remains distinct from online WAIT, and search has no execution authority.

Bounded autoresearch ladder#

This ladder estimates and gates research loops; it is not authorization to run them. One screening-run unit (SRU) is one fixed 12-update, two-seed arm. Under the recent 4,800-sample/update contract that is 115,200 sampled environment steps. A two-seed screen can reject but cannot promote. If the accepted frozen recipe uses a different batch size, recompute the ledger rather than preserving the number.

Global objective and invariants#

Primary evidence remains held-out chronological exact-1-bp net return and Sharpe, paired block-aware uplift, strongest static-direction control, turnover, and the existing 98% near-flat veto. A policy that is at least 98% short or long is not automatically rejected, but it must beat the matching static-direction control and demonstrate conditional state dependence. Fresh replay, lineage, finite metrics, and no sealed-test access are mandatory. Each loop changes one factor. PBT is off until factor attribution closes.

Autoresearch decision rule#

Every completed loop must emit exactly one of:

  • accept for replication: passed causal/mechanical and economic screen gates;
  • reject: failed a predeclared gate with valid evidence; or
  • defer: measurement or prerequisite is invalid, with the missing contract named explicitly.

Do not turn “promising training curve,” critic fit, predictive accuracy, Elo-like internal score, or proposer novelty into an economic promotion claim.

Transferable ideas, summarized#

Strong transfers#

  • keep task generation and task solving as distinct processes with explicit feedback and independently auditable state;
  • use learning progress rather than raw difficulty as a curriculum target;
  • compare adaptive curricula to matched fixed distributions over the same typed task space;
  • retain fresh, replayed, and mutated tasks while explicitly protecting diversity;
  • evaluate later proposer checkpoints by the value of frozen corpora to fresh learners;
  • isolate EMA, advantage filtering, and dynamic damping as PPO factors;
  • use a separate calibrated predictive model only when its target and causal meaning are explicit;
  • refine only the present action distribution during eventual search, leaving network weights and deterministic online authorities untouched.

Conditional transfers#

  • action/history features only when they carry causal information not already in the observation or explicit portfolio state;
  • distributional value only after critic diagnostics justify a separate value experiment;
  • GPU simulator redesign only after profiling shows simulator/reset/history materialization is the bottleneck;
  • learned test-time search only after a causal scenario generator and calibrated blueprint exist.

Non-transferable assumptions and explicit rejections#

  • Reject market-as-player: market data is not another copy of the solver.
  • Reject zero-sum equivalence: trading reward is not a symmetric terminal game outcome.
  • Reject “future actions determine the present” literally: only a policy- induced distribution over possible futures enters value.
  • Reject belief/world-model conflation: posterior latent state, future prediction, and known portfolio state are different objects.
  • Reject future leakage: realized future bars, labels, or future-derived regime_tags never enter the online observation.
  • Reject raw 32-action copy: present Family 00 target actions do not reveal exogenous market state in historical price-taking replay.
  • Reject direct search today: one realized future path is not a generative counterfactual market simulator.
  • Reject universal synthetic market replacement: contingent market facts must come from real observations.
  • Reject one-shot multi-factor adoption: damping, filtering, EMA, belief, proposer learning, S-series, recurrence, and R4-v2 stay separately attributable.
  • Reject logits as capability: CapabilityProfile requires empirical held-out evidence.

Open questions that must be answered before implementation#

  1. Which frozen solver recipe/checkpoint will serve as the learner for AZR-LP-DIRECT-v0 after current R-series attribution closes?
  2. What is the smallest independent capability panel that measures genuine transfer rather than improvement on the candidate task itself?
  3. Should proposer utility be signed economic uplift, a constrained vector of net/Sharpe/turnover/drawdown, or a Pareto admission rule? Absolute gradient alignment must not decide this policy question implicitly.
  4. Can task-gradient sketches predict direct micro-update uplift across solver checkpoints and seeds after normalizing reward scale?
  5. Which typed mutation fields preserve task validity and chronological eligibility without turning start index or seed into an identity shortcut?
  6. What QD descriptors promote meaningful curriculum coverage rather than merely filling bins?
  7. Is the current critic demonstrably miscalibrated enough to justify a distributional value experiment?
  8. What latent variable, if any, would make “market belief” a well-defined posterior rather than a renamed forecast?
  9. Can any future market scenario model beat simple causal baselines under multi-horizon proper scores, tail coverage, and regime shift before being used for action search?
  10. What total wall-clock, proposal, JVP, evaluation, and solver-step cost is acceptable per unit of curriculum uplift?

Recommended order#

  1. Close G0: repair/capture critic, clip-fraction, advantage, KL, action-margin, and exact fresh-panel replay receipts.
  2. Make recurrent lifecycle and evaluation state-correct; this is a mechanics prerequisite, not a new solver wing.
  3. Finish the fixed R-series boundary—matched R0, clean R1, and R1 + R4-v1—then run the existing R2c-RESET + R4-v1 versus R2c-CARRY + R4-v1 memory attribution, without modifying R4-v2's fixed design.
  4. Add ATX-EMA-EVAL-v0 as an evaluation-only experiment.
  5. Only with trustworthy critic/advantage telemetry, run the LR/regularization schedule arms and the all/random/top-|advantage| filter arms separately on the frozen accepted solver recipe, with PBT off.
  6. Build AZR-LP-DIRECT-v0 on a tiny validated TaskSpec set and test whether repeated local learning progress is measurable at all.
  7. Validate gradient-alignment surrogates against that direct reference before training an adaptive proposer.
  8. Add fixed quality-diversity replay before adaptive quality-diversity selection.
  9. Keep C0/C1/shuffled-C1 intact; develop PF as a separate calibrated axis, cross an accepted PF model with a solver, and measure the hindsight-search ceiling before paying to build a world model.
  10. Validate WM separately. Leave learned TTS and Market-JEPA deferred and independent until their own causal generative-model evidence exists; shadow-mode TTS is last.

The architectural opportunity is real: a fast numerical solver, a typed non-LLM proposer, and a measured learning-progress curriculum could form a far more complete AZR-inspired loop. The disciplined route is to use Ataraxos for training-control and later search principles, use the zero-data paper for curriculum credit assignment, and refuse the tempting but false equivalence between a hidden-piece game and historical financial markets.
