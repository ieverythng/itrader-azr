2. Scientific claim and calendar eligibility

Training dataset-v2 starts in 2022. Comparing its trained solvers on 2020-2021 therefore measures backward-time, cross-period representation robustness. Later training information, retained symbol selection, and split adjustment prevent a historical trading-deployment interpretation. This study does not establish forward-time economic generalization. A later prospective validation requires its own chronological source and protocol.

Preserve the approved 32 distinct symbol/session anchors, at least two per symbol, plus eight assigned by the declared metadata-only algorithm/seed; cross each anchor with horizons 96/128/160/200. The decision offset is 60 or 120 minutes after RTH open, with continuous same-session 60-bar lookback and full 200-bar future support. Exclude semantic [start-60,start+200) windows that intersect adaptive or uncertain exposure, including the approved adjacent-boundary purge. Keep UTC/session and calendar-close checks explicit.

Distinct symbol/session anchors do not make contemporaneous symbols independent. The four horizons intentionally overlap; cross-symbol calendar overlap is allowed and must enter dependence treatment. Same-date all-twelve availability is a diagnostic, not an extra eligibility gate. Non-overlap means separation from prohibited exposure; it does not promise independent market observations.

3. Recommended first confirmatory endpoint

Recommend one representation contrast under the fixed 1 bp training arm: R1 + R4-v1 minus R0 + R4-v1, using predetermined terminal checkpoints, deterministic 1 bp evaluation, and the existing Q24 arithmetic terminal net return including entry/switch/liquidation costs. This holds explicit state and cost curriculum fixed while comparing the market encoder. It is a proposal; the sixteen-cell study and its other comparisons remain available descriptively.

For training seed k, symbol s, eligible anchor a, horizon h, let d[k,s,a,h] be the paired difference in episode net return. With n_s anchors:

theta_k = (1/12) sum_s (1/n_s) sum_a (1/4) sum_h d[k,s,a,h]
theta_fixed_pair = (theta_seed1 + theta_seed2) / 2

Recommend theta_fixed_pair as the sole confirmatory endpoint, conditional on these two frozen trained checkpoint pairs and the declared calendar design. Display both theta_k estimates and their calendar uncertainty; opposite signs must remain visible. The proposed superiority hypothesis is theta_fixed_pair <= 0 versus > 0. This is neither a claim about the population of training seeds nor an absolute profitability/promotion gate. Q25 net-bps-per-bar, Sharpe, horizon slices, clean R1/R0, state/curriculum interactions, all five behavioral controls, turnover/drawdown, and attribution remain descriptive unless added to an explicitly expanded confirmatory family before outcomes.

The Q24 reporter already uses horizon -> anchor -> symbol -> equal-symbol weighting; Q26 must retain it. The selected stronger of long/short is a descriptive oracle choice: confirmation against directional controls requires both contrasts in the declared family, rather than choosing one after seeing its return.

4. Joint dependence and candidate methods

Use one shared calendar draw or multiplier for all paired policies, twelve symbols, four horizons, both frozen training seeds, and any declared contrasts. Keep horizons attached to their anchor. Build metadata components from overlaps of full maximal windows on actual exchange dates; record chronological session gaps as well as overlap. Separated components can still have serial dependence. Do not compress widely separated sparse anchors into an apparently dense daily series, insert zero outcomes for unobserved dates, independently resample symbols, or omit missing symbols and silently renormalize Q24 weights. Repeatedly rejecting incomplete draws until all symbols appear changes the resampling law; any such conditioning must be declared and calibrated.

Block construction must follow the declared trading-session geometry, not just row order. Report unique dates, component count/size/gaps, symbol coverage, maximum component share of fixed Q24 weight, admissible blocks, and whether every replicate retains the target. No universal minimum block count or bandwidth is adopted. Short blocks can miss dependence; long blocks can remove effective replication. Candidate methods and reference distributions must be registered before calibration. Kunsch, Shao and Politis, and MacKinnon et al. motivate these limitations; the sparse twelve-symbol adaptation is our proposal.

5. Calibration and ordered decisions

After exposure clearance and separately authorized sealed acquisition, lock one metadata-only candidate layout for calibration. Overlay synthetic paired returns using the exact Q24 weights and calendar geometry. Include serial dependence, common-factor/cross-symbol shocks, overlapping horizon outcomes, heterogeneous scales, heavy tails/asymmetry, and combined stress families. State stationarity assumptions and include a period-change sensitivity; no resampler automatically solves distribution shift.

Before simulations, humans must declare alpha, interval coverage target, Monte Carlo precision/replications, independent RNG streams, error tolerances, support/numerical failure rules, and how performance is aggregated across null families. A decision-ready candidate criterion is: every required null family must satisfy the declared Type-I and coverage tolerances with Monte Carlo uncertainty, preserve the estimand/support, and avoid declared numerical or bandwidth-instability failures. Keep invalid replicates in the requested-count ledger; a failed family cannot be dropped. Infinite-variance scenarios require an explicit incompatibility disposition rather than pretending a mean/variance procedure has passed. Morris et al. provide the simulation reporting framework. Report success-conditional error/coverage separately from total failure rates. Mean-zero population truth must not be replaced by forcing every realized synthetic sample's Q24 estimate to zero, which would suppress sampling variation.

Method development on a calibration battery requires a versioned change log and a predeclared independent confirmation battery. A method that fails cannot be rescued by attractive simulated-effect power; report power separately under predeclared nonzero effects. If no registered method qualifies, retain Q26_UNRESOLVED_FOR_LAYOUT, report descriptive effects, and return the next design decision to GRILL. Do not redraw dates or optimize tolerances to pass.
