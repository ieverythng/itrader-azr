03

Cost: Rate, Turnover And Episode Length

Current unit-position accounting uses prior position p, target q, next-bar return r and one-way per-unit-turnover rate c:

continuation gross = q * r
switching cost    = c * abs(q - p)
continuation net  = q * r - c * abs(q - p)
terminal net      = continuation net - c * abs(q)  [if liquidation applies]

c=1e-4 is 1 bp per turnover unit. It is not 1 bp per round trip or a per-bar holding charge. Diagnostic economic net is not interchangeable with scaled/shaped PPO optimizer reward; confirm that reward separately.

Independent accounting examples, not market results. All paths start flat.
Position pathTurnover, including liquidationCost at 1 bp
Long held for 200 decisions, then terminal liquidation22 bp
Long, long, short, short, then terminal liquidation44 bp
Flat throughout00 bp

The advisory's 4.4-bp gap is not numerically smaller than a 1-bp rate. Aggregate return differences and per-turnover rates also have different denominators. This assessment neither re-scores that historical gap nor infers its uncertainty.

Frozen-trace repricing

Additive net is G - c*U: gross return G and turnover U are fixed, including applicable terminal liquidation. The response is affine and cannot improve with higher costs.

Break-even is 10,000*G/U bp for U>0. A negative rate means no nonnegative cost makes the trace profitable; U=0 leaves the division undefined. Compounded wealth requires separately derived accounting.

Training at different costs

Separately trained policies can change both G and U. That is a different question requiring matched budgets, seeds and panels.

Cost sensitivity cannot by itself identify architecture, optimizer, exploration, sampling, leakage or directional exposure as the cause. SIP identifies a market-data feed, not a verified fill/spread/fee model.

Proposed public integration scenario. Literal entry/hold/flip/exit paths, terminal costs exactly once and frozen-trace repricing invariance. Reuse EpisodeEvidence and economic diagnostics; do not add another accounting owner.

WAIT / EXIT: Intent Versus Transition

Current actions target short -1, flat 0 or long +1. Repeating the target holds; choosing flat closes exposure. Time advances and the position still earns next-bar PnL.

Transition truth table: WAIT means keep position; EXIT means target zero.
Current positionHold / proposed WAITFlatten / proposed EXIT
ShortSHORT, turnover 0FLAT, turnover 1
FlatFLAT, turnover 0FLAT, turnover 0
LongLONG, turnover 0FLAT, turnover 1

Under those definitions WAIT and EXIT alias existing transitions. Five logits still change exploration/entropy, action probabilities and checkpoint dimensions, even with unchanged returns. Describing FLAT as only holding an already-flat account leaves exposed-state behavior undefined and must not silently remove the close action.

A distinct Phase C or portfolio intent needs validity/masking, position ownership, duration, risk authority, fill handling and already-flat behavior. Broker semantics stay in deterministic DecisionGate/RiskEngine/Executor shells. Online WAIT/NO_ACTION remains first-class without five training logits; matching family output widths does not confer matching semantics.

Separate factor, not a shortcut. Any future action change needs its own transition table and strict action-schema/restore tests. Do not mix it into R0/R1 x solver state or add trade quotas to manufacture activity.

DEV identifies a prospective restore gap: selector/config and output width are bound, but no explicit action-ID vocabulary is recorded. A same-width semantic remap needs its own version and rejection oracle before a future change; no remap or current runtime defect was established here.

Causal eligibility, not arbitrary gaps

T=60 is 60 one-minute bars, not two hours. A 240-bar history plus a 200-decision horizon cannot fit wholly in a 390-bar full RTH session. Check exact indexing and shortened sessions. Larger T can change the eligible panel, not just compute; surviving anchors are not automatically matched samples.

Evaluation And The One-Bar Diagnostic

Evidence validity, metric availability, uncertainty qualification and promotion eligibility are separate. Keep valid negative or inconclusive returns visible; quarantine invalid comparisons rather than replacing them with zero.

DEV19 still requires its execution-backed Spec review. This advisory does not close it.

Q26 criteria retained; qualification remains open

The accepted primary is fixed-1-bp paired terminal net uplift, not every reported statistic. The nominal 95% equal-tailed candidate, simultaneous MC coverage lower bound >=94% and directional-error upper bounds <=3% are unchanged. Process support, operational method and independent confirmation remain open. A small historical gap cannot predict whether the interval crosses zero.

One bar can diagnose accounting, not realized Sharpe

Bind action, prior position, causal market history and any optional volatility estimate before the next return. Report gross, switching cost, net and matched benchmark-relative net; distinguish continuation from terminal liquidation. Overlapping histories, calendars and positions prevent treating observations as independent samples.

One outcome has no meaningful realized Sharpe. An ex-post ratio needs a return series, stated reference, sampling period and variance treatment; see Sharpe's original treatment. Optional volatility normalization stays off and does not block net reporting.

Horizon-local attribution

directional = mean signed position * matched long-only gross
timing      = policy gross - directional
net         = directional + timing - costs

Cancellation in the net identity is intentional accounting. Do not clip attribution or select the best directional control after seeing returns. Timing is not automatically forecasting skill or independent of exposure. An ex-post best long/short control is a diagnostic oracle, not a deployable benchmark or replacement for predeclared paired-net inference.

New Wing: The Adversarial LLM Examiner

Pilot design only: a bounded conversational probe between deterministic contract validation and complete proposer-solver E2E testing. It examines causal fit, constraints under follow-up, uncertainty and demonstrated-defect correction.

It cannot certify economic performance, data freshness or statistical uplift. LM vs LM provides a factual-QA cross-examination precedent, not trading-task evidence. Scalable oversight distinguishes debate from consultancy with task-dependent results; this single-proposer examination is not two-sided debate. JudgeDeceiver motivates treating evaluated text as an injection surface. Local transfer remains a hypothesis.

Reuse current harness identities in the proposed evidence flow:

  1. Frozen fixture + causal summary + task contract
  2. Proposer invocation and initial candidate
  3. Deterministic validation and executable oracle
  4. Blinded static judge OR bounded examiner dialogue
  5. Final candidate validated again + complete dialogue receipt
  6. Defect-detection / false-rejection / cost report
Initial defect

Was the defect present in the first candidate?

Examiner detection

Did the examiner discover that defect?

Repair after feedback

Was the final candidate corrected?

Keep these endpoints distinct: persuasive explanations or repairs can hide poor initial proposals. Model size is a selection factor, not an intelligence guarantee. Qualify on held-out labeled defects, varied model families and orderings, with deterministic or independently reviewed ground truth.

Replayable pilot design

Compare deterministic-only, static rubric judge and bounded multi-turn examiner on the same fixtures. Include valid tasks, malformed payloads, plausible but incompatible horizons, fabricated summary claims, leakage requests, adversarial instructions and no-solution cases.

Measure detection, false rejection, abstention, stability and tokens/latency. Declare turn/token/tool limits and stop rules before calls. No numerical budget is selected here.

Restricted evidence, untrusted dialogue

No future outcomes, secrets, broker tools or approval rights. Quarantine failures and retain invocation/model/harness/fixture identities. Separate examiner development fixtures, held-out assessment fixtures and golden-task selection data.

Larger-model, same-family and cross-family arms are possible ablations, not mandatory orders-of-magnitude scaling. The dedicated examiner note supplies source-backed risks and pilot decisions. No calls, LoRA or golden-dataset promotion run here.

Canonical contract and access boundary. CapabilityProfile is a canonical future online/shadow contract with an existing typed scaffold, not an optional examiner role. Calibration requires separately permitted evidence. terminal_screen_only outcomes cannot feed operational calibration, proposer reward, curriculum, PPO or tuning. A conversational score cannot bypass those restrictions.
