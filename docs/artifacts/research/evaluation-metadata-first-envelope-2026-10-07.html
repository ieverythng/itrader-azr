All-twelve-symbol support: known versus unknown

The fixed universe remains:

AAPL, MSFT, AMZN, NVDA, AMD, AVGO, GOOGL, META, SPY, QQQ, IWM, TLT

The retained v2 metadata provides a useful qualification baseline without making v2 eligible for the new terminal role. All twelve symbols have rows on all 1,184 exchange dates through the snapshot end, but minute completeness differs by symbol. Across all twelve, 622 v2 dates support both accepted decision offsets with a continuous 60-bar lookback and 200-bar longest outcome. None of the nine early closes supports either full all-twelve offset. Those facts show why calendar membership alone is insufficient and why no forward fill is permitted. Same-date all-twelve completeness is a useful diagnostic, not a panel-selection requirement: the accepted contract is 32 distinct symbol/session anchors across all twelve symbols, with at least two per symbol.

For the recommended 2020-2021 envelope, only venue-calendar facts are known: 505 sessions, 6,060 requested symbol/session slots, and three early closes. Actual rows, symbol-session presence, gaps, duplicates, complete 260-minute windows, and offset eligibility are unknown until a declared sealed acquisition. The metadata-only post-ingestion audit must report all of them for every symbol. Failure is reported; the interval is not silently widened and missing bars are not imputed.

The complete per-symbol v2 comparison is retained in the calendar receipt. It is context for the validator, not a candidate terminal panel.

Draft bounded retrospective-clearance standard

The proposed bounded_retrospective_clearance_v1 is deliberately stronger than date non-overlap and weaker than an impossible claim of omniscience. It would permit a historical envelope only when all of the following are recorded:

  1. Semantic non-overlap: every full [start-60, start+200) symbol/UTC window lies outside every retained training, validation, final-test, proposer, tuning, calibration, checkpoint-selection, diagnostic, plot, and manually inspected value-bearing interval.
  2. All known sources: the scan covers dataset-v2, legacy dataset-v1, every retained experiment/config root, recovered and unresolved exposure records, repository history, local analysis artifacts, and declared external tools or copies. Legacy-v1 is not omitted merely because v2 superseded it.
  3. Uncertainty is exclusionary: an unresolved source that might overlap the proposed dates blocks clearance. Start-unbound randomness cannot be narrowed from a convenient seed reconstruction, while an exact dataset identity may still bound the dates that randomness could have touched.
  4. Consumer completeness: the ledger names training, progress monitoring, proposer/curriculum, tuning/PBT, calibration, checkpoint choice, diagnostics, plots, human inspection, agents, and later online/capability consumers.
  5. Human boundary statement: the owner records whether any unlogged chart, notebook, broker UI, external drive, or prior copy exposed 2020-2021 values. Filesystem absence cannot answer this on its own.
  6. Append-only continuation: declaration, acquisition, metadata selection, Q26 calibration, terminal opening, and every rejected/failed attempt enter one access ledger. Hashes establish identity and drift; they do not prove truth.

The present evidence supports proposing 2020-2021 for clearance review because it is disjoint from known exact-hash v1/v2 file dates. In particular, the 42 unresolved v2 inventory records are source-bound to dataset-v2; their uncertainty does not reach backward into 2020-2021. Clearance still has not passed: 120 older run configs bind only a canonical path without a historical hash, three retained configs name a now-absent external checkout, twelve task pools and six candidate ledgers lack dataset/time coordinates, and the human boundary statement cannot be supplied by filesystem evidence. No report can prove that an unretained external copy never existed.

Stop rules

Stop without fallback or reinterpretation if:

  • the retrospective-clearance review or human boundary statement is incomplete;
  • the DEV role/certificate seam has not landed and passed independent review;
  • the first sealed explicit-SIP request fails authentication/entitlement, returns a mismatched feed, defaults to another feed, or would require a paid upgrade;
  • any provider, feed, timeframe, adjustment, universe, date, or session policy differs from the declaration;
  • raw request/response retention, retrieval time, usage classification, or hashes are missing;
  • metadata finds duplicate/reversed timestamps, cross-session windows, or insufficient eligible anchors to satisfy the frozen 32-anchor/twelve-symbol rule or the later predeclared Q26 calendar-support gate;
  • a prohibited consumer or human opens value-bearing data before authorization;
  • no predeclared candidate Q26 procedure qualifies on the locked candidate layout under the approved synthetic calibration contract; or
  • anyone proposes redrawing dates, dropping a difficult symbol/null family, or widening the envelope in place after seeing calibration or market results.

A failed declaration is never edited in place to make a result pass. If its predeclared metadata support fails, or no candidate procedure qualifies on its locked layout, GRILL may authorize a genuinely new declaration only with an independent support rationale, preserved failed receipt, new hash/layout lock, and complete recalibration. A method-specific failure is preserved against that method, not mislabeled as proof that the layout itself is inadequate. This is not permission to iterate envelopes until a preferred result appears.

Q26 synthetic-calibration boundary

The approved sequence permits candidate-method work on synthetic outcomes after the envelope and metadata layout are frozen. It does not approve a method or numbers. The Q26 calibration contract requires two distinct studies:

  1. Synthetic null calibration: empirical Type-I error, interval coverage, numerical/support failure, and stability under predeclared serial, cross-symbol, within-anchor/horizon, heavy-tail/asymmetric, heterogeneous- scale, and combined dependence families.
  2. Separately named simulated-effect power: rejection probability under predeclared nonzero synthetic Q24 effects, reported with Monte Carlo uncertainty and never described as solver profitability or expected return.

Every null family, method attempt, invalid replicate, and failure remains in the ledger. Power cannot rescue failed null calibration. The procedure may not tune panel dates, remove a failing null, change block settings until Type-I/coverage gates pass, later change them until terminal significance appears, or select the narrowest interval. If no predeclared candidate procedure qualifies on the locked layout, the neutral result is Q26_UNRESOLVED_FOR_LAYOUT; GRILL decides what happens next without opening market outcomes.

Still-open numerical decisions include the confirmatory contrast family, calendar unit, candidate methods, synthetic generators/parameters, block or bandwidth rule, interval construction, alpha, coverage/error tolerances, replication count, RNG streams/seeds, multiplicity family, support/failure thresholds, and the limited simulated-effect grid. Two training seeds remain a descriptive mechanics/attribution screen, not population-level inference.

Limitations

  • No bar endpoint was called, no bar value was read, and no acquisition occurred.
  • Current SIP entitlement remains unknown; historical old-SIP success is not a plan or pricing claim.
  • Venue sessions do not prove per-symbol bar completeness or eligible anchors.
  • The retained exposure inventory is bounded and contains unresolved records; it cannot certify deleted, remote, or manually viewed material.
  • Because dataset-v2 begins in 2022, this pre-v2 block is a backward-time, cross-period retrospective representation screen. It is not prospective forward-time validation, a live-economic promotion gate, or evidence that the selected solver will generalize to current markets. Its temporal/provider shift must remain explicit in every interpretation.
  • Synthetic calibration can reject bad inference procedures but cannot prove its generators match markets, create training-seed replication, or establish a profitable solver.
  • The recommended interval, role implementation, snapshot, panel, Q26 method, terminal execution, and any operational capability inference all remain uncompleted.
