**2. Full calendar geometry — a distinct metadata receipt.**
Bind the complete ordered exchange calendar, including unselected open sessions,
and canonical local trading dates. Report occupied-date incidence, session and
civil-day spans/gaps, shared-date anchors, closures, early closes and missingness/

[NYSE official hours](https://www.nyse.com/trade/hours-calendars) establish RTH
and closures, not the historical calendar certificate or a panel result.

**3. Weight concentration and horizon overlap — layout, not effective sample size.**
After fixed horizon/pair averaging, anchor weight is `q[s,a]=1/(12*n_s)`;
symbol totals remain `1/12`. A permitted frozen layout determines anchor/date
weight masses, concentration summaries and exact overlapping information windows.
Record same-date symbols and nested horizons together; four horizons and two
checkpoint pairs do not create independent calendar replications. A concentration
number, residual dimension or date count is not an adopted effective sample size.
The [reporter](../../../itrader/rl_lib/evaluation/terminal_panel_report.py) owns

**4. Distance support — coordinates now, candidate-dependent geometry later.**
Metadata could determine all session/civil-day distances and which sites occupy
each distance. Only after a kernel and bandwidth rule are specified can it show
their neighborhoods, disconnected groups and zero-support pairs. Same-date
coupling must remain joint across symbols/horizons/fixed pairs. Gaps are not
zero-return observations. This construction audit cannot identify true market
lag/decay, moments, covariance or adequate coverage. Inference distance, DGP clock,
kernel and bandwidth remain distinct; calendar is not a regime/outcome label.

**5. PSD and weighted projection — deterministic algebra conditional on a method.**
For an illustrative linear centering candidate only, let `P` be its centering
operator, `A` aggregate anchors to dates, `B=A diag(q) P`, and `K` the proposed
date-multiplier covariance. The design knows `B` and `B'KB` once the candidate is
declared; inspect symmetry, PSD, null spaces/rank and alignment with the residual
subspace. PSD alone permits zero quadratic forms. With symbol centering and
zero-sum date contributions, `K -> 11'` annihilates those contributions. This is
algebraic failure potential, not an observed panel failure or chosen centering.
Actual `x'B'KBx`, dispersion and interval behavior require outcomes (synthetic
ones only during separately authorized calibration), not metadata.
[GPML ch4](https://gaussianprocess.org/gpml/chapters/RW4.pdf) defines covariance
validity/PSD; the weighted-projection consequence is our derivation. Numeric
conditioning tolerances and rejection criteria remain open; not every legitimate
null direction or low rank warrants rejection. No matrix was calculated here.

**6. Statistical qualification — authorized synthetic calibration, not metadata.**
Evaluate the frozen complete procedure on the qualified layout with known
generator truth, both mandatory families/both DGP clocks, relevant strong cases
inside declared bounds, and the three distinct roles. Freeze scenarios and method
selection before confirmation, with independently frozen streams. Coverage,
directional errors, failures and inner/outer Monte Carlo precision cannot be
certified by calendar span, PSD or extra draws. A finite registry tests declared
points, not every intervening parameter or real-market assumption. No assumption
detector is promised for unobservable departures from the bounded class.

## Primary evidence and transfer limits

**Cited fact:** [Shao's DWB paper, assumptions and sections4-5](https://publish.illinois.edu/xshao/files/2012/11/JASA-DWB.pdf)
uses stationary weak dependence, moment/sampling conditions and bandwidth/domain
asymptotics for irregular locations. Its covariance construction requires validity.
**Inference:** actual geometry matters before layout-specific numerical rules;
the theorem does not qualify a sparse fixed-weight iTrader panel or supply a
universal minimum calendar span. It does not tell us the market's process range.

**Cited fact:** [Morris, White and Crowther](https://arxiv.org/abs/1712.03198)
organize simulation design around aims, generators, estimands, methods and
performance measures. **Inference:** freeze these with the actual layout and
preserve failed required cases; metadata may inform feasibility but cannot
justify trimming scientifically required persistence until calibration passes.

**Cited fact:** [NIST Bonferroni inequality](https://itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section4/prc473.htm)
applies to arbitrary events, and [NIST binomial limits](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/prc/section2/prc241.htm)
describe binomial interval construction. **Inference:** enumerate required
checks before choosing MC confidence/allocation/precision; the binomial experiment
assumptions still require justification. This adopts neither Bonferroni nor a
confidence value, failure ceiling or replication budget. Nominal market-interval
level and simultaneous calibration confidence are different quantities.

## Ordering alternatives and safeguards

**Metadata-first with parallel assumption drafting — recommended.** It avoids
numeric rules for an imaginary design and exposes missing sources/identities
early. Tradeoff: numerical closure must wait for separately authorized qualified
metadata. Draft scientific scope now, but do not infer warranted moments/decay
from dates or shrink required bounds to suit an inconvenient layout.

**Ranges-first.** Freezing scientifically justified finite bounds before layout
can guard against tailoring the class to a passing method. Tradeoff: without
qualified geometry it may be infeasible or require an explicit new design
decision; it cannot certify support. Nothing here supplies justified numbers.

**Method/calibration-first on an assumed layout.** It could later test software
mechanics if separately authorized, but would not qualify the real panel. It is
not the next recommended gate, and no simulation is authorized here.

No sequencing alternative permits postfailure relabeling of a required strong-
dependence case as a rejection/diagnostic case. Predeclared observable structural
defects can reject; latent persistence cannot simply be asserted detectable.
If qualified support is insufficient for the declared scientific scope, retain
the failure and valid descriptives, then return a separate redesign decision to
GRILL. Do not downgrade95/94/3, enlarge the panel, change weights or choose a
shorter lag automatically. Existing
[disposition](../../../itrader/rl_lib/evaluation/evaluation_disposition.py)
validates caller-supplied statuses; it does not derive statistical qualification.
The [legacy row-block routines](../../../itrader/rl_lib/evaluation/block_uncertainty.py)
remain exploratory, not a substitute for the accepted clock candidate.
