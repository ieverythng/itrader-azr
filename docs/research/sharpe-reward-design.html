iTrader / reward research

Sharpe-aware shaping, implemented as a bounded experiment.

PnL remains the only proven default. Sharpe-only and PnL+Sharpe are explicit challengers whose family assignment remains evidence-gated.

Updated 2026-09-03Default: pnl_linearChallengers: experimental

Decision

pnl_linear is the only reward proven in training and remains family 00's sole default. sharpe_only and the pnl_sharpe blend are selectable challengers.

score_t = tanh((mean(strategy_returns) / std(strategy_returns)) / target)
sharpe_only_t = w_sharpe * (score_t - score_(t-1))
pnl_sharpe_t = pnl_linear_t + sharpe_only_t

The incremental score avoids paying the same path-level Sharpe repeatedly. Optimization uses no minute-scale annualization, which would saturate the bounded signal.

Why It Is Compelling

  • Values consistency rather than terminal PnL alone.
  • Blends with dense PnL during estimator warmup.
  • Bounds low-volatility edge cases.
  • Exposes sharpe_delta in reward telemetry.

Why It Is Experimental

  • Serial correlation weakens naive Sharpe assumptions.
  • Short windows produce noisy dispersion estimates.
  • Breakout returns may be punished for useful asymmetry.
  • Aggregate training reward cannot establish held-out utility.

ITraderEnv owns episode strategy-return history; RewardEngine owns calculation and finite components. No broker or risk semantics enter TaskSpec.

Matched Evaluation

  1. Compare pnl_linear, sharpe_only, and the pnl_sharpe blend on SolverMLPFiLM.
  2. Run sharpe_reward_ablation.json; hold data, task seeds, PPO budget, horizon, and sampling mode constant.
  3. Report held-out return, Sharpe, drawdown, turnover, entropy, saturation, and confidence intervals.
  4. Repeat under the winning recurrent head only after the recurrent ablation is valid.

Reward-parameter sampling uses a separate RNG, so switching reward ID does not change sampled horizons or market-window seeds.

Do not add Sharpe-dominant or trend-persistence rewards until this shared reward demonstrates incremental value. Family 03 is trade management and needs a separate state contract.

Promotion Gate

Keep PnL as the sole default unless a challenger passes focused and full tests, three matched seeds without collapse, and held-out improvement without unacceptable loss of return or throughput.
