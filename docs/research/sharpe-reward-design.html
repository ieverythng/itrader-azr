Sharpe-Aware Reward Design

Last updated: 2026-09-12 Status: experimental challengers; pnl_linear remains the only proven/default reward

Decision summary

The reward seam is independent of recurrent architecture. Keep pnl_linear as the family 00 default. sharpe_only and pnl_sharpe are challengers that require matched held-out evidence. A recurrence implementation does not require changing reward, and a longer horizon is not automatically better.

The reward seam is environment-owned by RewardEngine. These challenger formulas are reward calculations, not learned reward heads, and recurrence/head selection must remain an independent decision.

score_t = tanh((mean(strategy_returns) / std(strategy_returns)) / target)
sharpe_delta_t = w_sharpe * (score_t - score_(t-1))
pnl_sharpe_t = pnl_linear_t + sharpe_delta_t

Warmup and effectively zero variance produce a finite zero contribution. The exact staged source change in itrader/rl_lib/tasks/rewards.py is pre-existing, staged, and outside this docs-only pass; do not infer that its current behavior has been reimplemented or revalidated here.

Families 00–04 are roadmap labels, not a settled shared-head architecture. Even if 00–02 use Discrete(3), their inputs, task semantics, reward assignments, and state ownership may differ and must be recorded explicitly.

Before clipping and with an undiscounted sum, deltas telescope:

sum_t(score_t - score_(t-1)) = score_T - score_0

That algebra does not make the shaped reward policy-invariant under discounted PPO (gamma < 1), clipping, finite warmup, changing episode boundaries, or other implementation details. Report exactly which sequence, discount, clipping, and terminal conventions were used. Do not call incremental Sharpe a guaranteed policy-preserving potential term.

No annualization is needed for optimization by default; minute-scale annualizing can saturate a bounded signal. Annualized values may be reported separately with the sampling convention stated. Sharpe remains sensitive to serial correlation, regime duration, overlap, estimator warmup, and episode boundaries. It is not automatically horizon-invariant.

Phase B includes both offline/curriculum work and online replay/shadow validation; Phase C is later operationalization. Proposer and solver/reward studies remain separate until the user's grill-me review resolves open architecture choices.

The local AZR v2 paper is not evidence that Sharpe shaping is required. Its Eq. 4 proposer reward is 0 at observed solve rate p=0 or p=1, otherwise 1-p. That is asymmetric and not a symmetric 50% peak or a measured learning-progress uplift. The paper's coding/math ablations are not iTrader returns.

iTrader uses historical OHLCV, a language-model proposer, and a numerical PPO solver. train_AZR.py currently performs task-pool/bootstrap handoff rather than a co-adaptive AZR loop. Proposer reference-buffer and frozen/adaptive studies belong in a separate experiment matrix, with validation feedback and an untouched final chronological test. Use a cost-aware verifier and report rejected tasks, generation calls, tokens/latency, compute, costs, turnover, and drawdown.

Matched reward study

Hold data eligibility, task identity, window, horizon, seeds, solver head, PPO budget, and evaluation split constant. Compare:

  • pnl_linear;
  • sharpe_only;
  • pnl_sharpe.

Start with R0. Recurrent comparisons follow the same reward cell rather than combining reward and architecture changes in the first pass. Track held-out return, Sharpe under a stated convention, max drawdown, turnover, costs, action entropy, reward-component saturation, finite losses, throughput, and confidence intervals across seeds. Family/regime claims require emitted task metadata; an aggregate episode-return metric is insufficient.

Keep the input window, episode horizon, BPTT/unroll (max_seq_len), and reward-statistics window distinct; Sharpe history is the last of these, not the episode horizon itself.

Recurrence asks whether a solver benefits from temporal input encoding and carried state. Sharpe shaping asks whether optimization values a bounded path statistic. Neither proves the other is necessary. Keep reward controls and R-series controls orthogonal until each has evidence.

Do not claim that a recurrent hidden state automatically tracks PnL, position, action history, or episode progress from OHLCV alone. If those are causal inputs, add them as explicit, normalized fields to both feed-forward and recurrent cells. Do not call logits/value outputs calibrated confidence or expected net utility without separate calibration and cost-aware economic evaluation.

Promotion gate

A challenger is promoted only when compatible-environment tests, at least three matched seeds, held-out chronological evaluation, finite behavior, action diversity, drawdown/turnover/cost criteria, and horizon/regime slices support it. Record the selected parameters in experiment artifacts; do not silently change a family default. No reward conclusion is reported by this documentation pass.
