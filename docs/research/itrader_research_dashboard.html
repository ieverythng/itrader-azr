Reward parity is an open decision

RewardEngine dispatches TaskSpec.reward_id. Family 00 and the repository retain pnl_linear as default. The corrected four-by-two-by-two draft and CUDA37 test explicitly use net_return; an evaluation/net_return_mean score does not select the training reward.

Commit 88361f0 (Sep 19) added the separate objective; f045fde retains the Sep-14 probe and Sep-16 matched reward bridge. The audit explicitly says not to silently change pnl_linear. Historical experiments are not proof of human approval to replace the default; Q22's optimizer recipe does not resolve the reward choice.

  • pnl_linear: normalized PnL, task-sampled abstract turnover/drawdown penalties and caps; its current function does not charge terminal liquidation.
  • net_return: fixed-scale PnL minus explicit basis-point switching and terminal liquidation costs; the draft fixes scale 1000. It is a different objective under the same economic evaluator.
  • Net-return sampling still varies symbols, horizons and task/start seeds; the env supplies stream starts. The draft fixes reward ID, scale and initial cost; ramp changes apply at subsequent episode resets.
  • PnL-linear sampling additionally varies supported turnover/drawdown knobs. semantic_v2 hashes reward ID/parameters; it is not a learned numeric reward encoder. Legacy encoding omits reward parameters.

RESEARCH-REWARD38 audits bounded pnl_linear repairs, Sharpe shaping/pure Sharpe and alternative objectives, including scale, critic, discount, state and accounting. A 50/50 episode-level mixture is only a secondary candidate, not approved training. Existing Sharpe design remains experimental; no reward behavior changed here.

The delivered REWARD38 primary-source audit identifies the next public-contract checks, not an adopted objective:

  • Preserve evaluation TaskSpec conditioning; independent 1-bp economic scoring does not require net-return TaskSpecs.
  • Audit adaptive PnL/drawdown scaling versus unscaled turnover, caps and terminal liquidation with literal accounting oracles.
  • Current Sharpe rewards use gross strategy history and rolling bounded score differences, not differential or cost-net Sharpe. Test reward history/state, warmup, near-zero variance, window eviction and terminal handling before a cost-aware supplement; pure Sharpe and mixtures follow later.
  • R1/R4 do not expose endogenous return/EMA/drawdown reward history. Reward-state additions are separate factors; these risks are not measured causes of the observed policy basin.
  • CPU and CUDA installed Ray sources agree: squared critic error is clamped, and advantages are standardized over policy batches, not separately per reward task. Static parity is not measured saturation or optimizer invariance. Exact file hashes are recorded in the Markdown source.
