AZR · proposer-credit and curriculum ablations

train_AZR.py currently generates and validates a task pool, then hands that fixed pool to PPO once. It is a one-shot bootstrap handoff, not the co-adaptive loop.

Core-loop boundary: proposer → validated TaskSpec/buffer → PPO solver → feedback/r_prop → proposer remains masterplan-owned. The cells below compare alternatives inside that planned loop; they do not replace it.
Original AZR proxy: r_prop = 0 at solve rate p = 0 or p = 1; otherwise r_prop = 1 - p. This is an asymmetric learnability proxy—not a parabola, not a symmetric peak at p = 0.5, and not direct measured learning progress.

Cross-axis discipline

Admissible after prerequisites

  • R1 + R4-v1 — window encoder × endogenous state
  • R2c-CARRY + R4-v1 vs identical RESET
  • accepted solver × C1, with shuffled-C1
  • accepted solver × PF1, with no/zero/shuffle controls
  • S1-P3 ranker × fixed solver recipe from pinned starting checkpoints and equal accepted compute

Never conflate

  • R4-v1 with memory, belief, PF, or WM
  • R4-v2 with PF, WM, TTS, or extra state fields
  • C1 with recurrence evidence
  • CLM/Jev with Market-JEPA or “LLM-free” evidence
  • PPO logits/value with empirical capability
  • solver flat with online WAIT
  • historical replay with a counterfactual world model
  • PBT activity with economic promotion
Replay/shadow boundary: the control flow, typed online seams, WAIT/no-match behavior, capability evidence, and deterministic gate/risk/executor ownership are defined by the architecture and ordered by the masterplan. They are prerequisites for later research cells, not duplicated dashboard wings.
